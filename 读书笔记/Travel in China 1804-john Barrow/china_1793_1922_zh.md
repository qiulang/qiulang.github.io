# 第一部分 总纲:全书论断之骨干十段

下列十段是巴罗全书的承重结构。第一、二两段是读者所选,列在最前——它们是同一个论断的**原理面**与**实证面**;第三段是这一机制的设计说明。四、五两段是全书立论。六至八段是政治结构的三处断裂。第九段是文明判词。第十段是全书唯一的对照组,也是它最有力的一段。

---

## 一、p. 179 受杖谢恩,人之尊严尽失(原理)

> In a government, where every man is liable to be made a slave, where every man is subject to be flogged with the bamboo at the nod of one of the lowest rank of those in office, and where he is compelled to kiss the rod that beats him or, which amounts to the same thing, to thank the tyrant on his knees for the trouble he has taken to correct his morals, high notions of honour and dignified sentiments are not to be expected. Where the maxims of the government commanding, and the opinions of the people agreeing, that corporal punishment may be inflicted, on the ground of a favour conferred upon the person punished, a principle of humiliation is admitted that is well calculated to exclude and obliterate every notion of the dignity of human nature.

——〔译〕在这样一个政体之下:人人都可能被贬为奴隶;人人都可能因最低微的一名官吏点一点头而被竹板笞责;受责之后还被迫去亲吻那打他的棍子——或者说,这是一回事——跪下来感谢那个暴虐者不惜费心矫正他的品行。在这样的政体里,是不可能指望有什么高尚的荣誉观念与有尊严的情感的。当政府的准则如此规定、而民众的意见又与之一致,认为体罚可以施加、并且是施加于受责者的一种恩惠,那么一条屈辱的原则就被**接受**了下来;而这条原则,恰好足以排除并抹去一切关于人之尊严的观念。

要点在 "or, which amounts to the same thing":亲吻鞭子与跪谢矫正,是同一件事的仪式与语法。惩罚一旦被重新描述为恩惠,受刑者就必须用感激来配合这个描述——屈辱于是从外部转入内部。巴罗的用词是 **admitted**(被接受),不是 imposed(被施加)。

## 二、p. 381–382 沿途所见,满汉之别(实证)

> In travelling through the country, a day seldom escaped without our witnessing the application of the Pan-tsé or bamboo, and generally in such a manner that it might be called by any other name except a gentle correction. A Chinese suffering under this punishment cries out in the most piteous manner; a Tartar bears it in silence. A Chinese, after receiving a certain number of strokes, falls down on his knees, as a matter [382] of course, before him who ordered the punishment, thanking him, in the most humble manner, for the fatherly kindness he has testified towards his son, in thus putting him in mind of his errors; a Tartar grumbles, and disputes the point as to the right that a Chinese may have to flog him; or he turns away in sullen silence.

——〔译〕我们一路行经各地,几乎没有一天不亲见板子——即笞杖——的施用,而其施用的样子,你可以给它取任何名目,唯独不能叫作"温和的矫正"。一个汉人受这种刑罚时,呼号之声最为可怜;一个旗人则默然承受。汉人挨过一定数目的板子之后,照例跪倒在下令责打他的人面前,以最卑顺的口吻谢他对"儿子"所施的父亲般的慈爱,谢他如此提醒自己的过错;旗人则口出怨言,还要争辩一个汉人究竟有什么权利来打他,或者索性板着脸转身不理。

这是巴罗无意中留下的对照实验:同一根板子、同一套规矩,汉人跪谢"as a matter of course",旗人抗辩甚至质问"一个汉人凭什么打我"。若屈辱出于天性,两者应当一致;既不一致,变量便不是血统,而是位置——征服者与被征服者。巴罗本人用此证明汉人更堕落,但他记下的事实比他的结论更诚实。

## 三、p. 382–383 举国奴颜婢膝——机制的设计说明

> Ridiculous as it may appear to a foreigner, in observing an officer of state stretching himself along the ground for the purpose of being flogged by order of another who happens to rank one degree above him; yet it is impossible, at the same time, to suppress a glow of indignation, in witnessing so mean and obsequious a degradation of the human mind, which can bring itself, under any circumstances, patiently to submit to a vile corporal punishment, administered by the hand of a slave, or by a common soldier; and when this is done, to undergo the still more vile and humiliating act of kissing the rod that corrects him. But the policy of the government has taken good care to remove any scruples that might arise on this score. Where paternal regard was the sole motive, such a chastisement could not possibly be followed with dishonour or disgrace. It was a wonderful point gained by the government, to subject every individual, the Emperor only excepted, to the same corporal correction; but it must have required great address, and men's minds must have been completely subdued, or completely convinced, before such a system of universal obedience could have been accomplished, the consequence of which, it was obvious, could be no other than universal servility. It could not fail to establish a most effectual check against the complaints of the multitude, by shewing them that [383] the same man, who had the power of punishing them, was equally liable to be corrected in his turn, and in the same manner, by another.

——〔译〕一个朝廷大员匍匐在地,只因另一个恰好比他高一级的人下令,便要受笞——在外国人看来,这固然可笑;然而目睹人的心智如此卑贱驯顺地堕落,又实在无法按下心中的一股愤慨:他竟能在任何情形下都耐心地俯首于一种下贱的体罚,而行刑者只是一个奴仆或一名寻常兵丁;受责之后,还要再做一件更下贱、更屈辱的事——亲吻那矫正他的棍子。但政府的策略早已细心地把这上头可能生出的一切顾忌都清除掉了:既然唯一的动机是父母之慈爱,这样的惩戒就绝不可能带来耻辱或不名誉。政府所赢得的一个了不起的着数,是让除皇帝以外的每一个人都同受这一种体罚;但这必定需要极大的手腕,而且人心必定已被彻底压服、或彻底说服,这样一套普遍服从的制度才可能成立——其后果显而易见,只能是普遍的奴性。它也必然成为一道最有效的闸门,足以堵住众人的怨言:因为它让众人看到,那个有权责打他们的人,自己同样随时可能以同样的方式被别人责打。

他用的词是 policy、address、system——全是制度语言。这段说明"跪谢"不是民性,是设计:一旦人人可打、上级亦随时可被更上一级打,受刑者便连抱怨的立场都失去了。

## 四、p. 160 天性与制度之别(立论)

> I have already had occasion to observe that the natural disposition of the Chinese should seem to have suffered almost a total change by the influence of the laws and maxims of government, an influence which, in this country more than elsewhere, has given a bias to the manners, sentiments, and moral character of the people; for here every ancient proverb carries with it the force of a law. While they are by nature quiet, passive, and timid, the state of society and the abuse of the laws by which they are governed, have rendered them indifferent, unfeeling, and even cruel, as a few examples, which among many others occurred, will but too clearly bear evidence.

——〔译〕我前面已经有机会指出:中国人天生的性情,似乎因法律与政府准则的影响而几乎全然改易;这种影响在此国比在别处更甚,它使这个民族的举止、情感与道德品性都发生了偏斜——因为在这里,每一句古老的成语都带着法律的效力。他们生性安静、被动而胆怯;然而社会的状况与治理他们的那些法律之被滥用,却使他们变得冷漠、麻木,甚至残忍;下面要举的几个例子(还有许多别的),会把这一点证明得再清楚不过。

全书的立论起手式:**天性**(by nature)与**后果**(rendered)被明确分开,中间的变量是 the state of society and the abuse of the laws。这是整部书不能被读成种族论的根据。

## 五、p. 359 权力塑造国民性

> At the present day, however, it is sufficiently evident, that the heavy hand of power has completely overcome and moulded to its own shape the physical character of the people, and that their moral sentiments and actions are swayed by the opinions, and almost under the entire dominion, of the government.

——〔译〕然而到了今天,有一点已经足够明显:权力那只沉重的手,已经彻底压服了这个民族的体质性格,并把它塑造成了权力自己的形状;他们的道德情感与行为,则受政府的意见左右,几乎完全处在政府的支配之下。

第四段的重述,位置在讲政府与法律的第七章开头——巴罗两次立论,两次都是这句话。

## 六、p. 396 民不知有其权

> Contented in having no voice in the government, it has never occurred to them that they have any rights: and they certainly enjoy none but what are liable to be invaded and trampled on, whenever the sovereign, or any of his representatives, from interest, malice, or caprice, think fit to exercise the power that is within their grasp. The doctrine of employing resistence against oppression, applied to the people and the government, is so contrary to every sentiment of the former, that the latter has little to fear on that score.

——〔译〕他们安于在政事上没有声音,从来没有想到过自己拥有任何权利;而他们确实也没有任何权利可享——除了那些随时可能被侵夺践踏的:只要君主或他的任何一个代表出于利害、恶意或一时任性,认为可以动用手中的权力,便动用了。至于以抵抗对付压迫这一道理,若用在民与政府之间,则与民众的一切情感如此相违,以致政府在这上头几乎无须忧虑。

这是全书最冷的一句。不是"无权利",而是**从未想到自己有权利**——压迫的最后一道工序,是让被压迫者连这个念头都不生。

## 七、p. 389 无中间阶层:只有治人者与治于人者

> The officers of government in general, though intended by the constitution as a kind of barrier between the prince and the people, are the greatest oppressors of the latter, who have seldom any means of redress, or of conveying their complaints to the Imperial ear. There is no middle class of men in China: men whose property and ideas of independence give them weight in the part of the country where they reside; and whose influence and interest are considered as not below the notice of the government. In fact, there are no other than the governors and the governed. If a man, by trade, or industry in his profession, has accumulated riches, he can enjoy them only in private. He dares not, by having a grander house, or finer clothes, to let his neighbour perceive that he is richer than himself, lest he should betray him to the commanding officer of the district, who would find no difficulty in bringing him within the pale of the sumptuary laws, and in laying his property under confiscation.

——〔译〕政府的官员按制度本意本应是君与民之间的一道屏障,实际上却是虐民最甚者;而民众极少有伸冤之途,也极少有办法把自己的苦情送到皇帝耳中。中国没有中间阶层——即那种因财产与独立意识而在其所居之地拥有分量、其影响与利益不至于被政府视为不值一顾的人。事实上,除了治人者与治于人者,别无他人。一个人若靠营商或勤于本业积下了财富,也只能在私下享用。他不敢盖更大的房子、穿更好的衣服,让邻人看出自己比他富有,免得被告发到本地长官那里去——长官要把他套进禁奢之律、把他的家产查抄入官,是毫无困难的。

结构性的诊断:没有能与权力讲价的第三方,只有两级。第八段所写的普遍欺诈,是这个结构的必然产物。

## 八、p. 179–180 奴隶不受辱:上下交征利

> A slave, in fact, cannot be dishonoured. The condition itself of being dependent upon and subject to the caprice of another, without the privilege of appeal, is such a degraded state of the human species, that those who are unfortunately reduced to it have no further ignominy or sense of shame to undergo. The vices of such a condition are innumerable, and they appear on all occasions among this people celebrated (rather undeservedly I think) for their polished manners and civilized government. A Chinese merchant will cheat, whenever an opportunity offers him the means, because he is considered to be incapable of acting honestly; a Chinese peasant will steal when ever he can do it without danger of being detected, because the punishment is only the bamboo, to which he is daily liable; and a Chinese prince, or a prime minister, will extort the property of the subject, and apply it to his private use, whenever he thinks he can do it with impunity. The only check upon the rapacity of men in power is the influence of fear, arising from the possibility of detection: the love of honour, the dread of shame, and a sense of justice, seem to be equally unfelt by the majority of men in office.

——〔译〕奴隶其实是不可能再被羞辱的。依附于他人、任他人一时之性、又无上诉之权,这种境况本身已是人类如此卑下的状态,以致不幸沦入其中的人,再没有什么屈辱或羞耻可承受了。这种境况的恶德不可胜数,而它们时时处处显现在这个民族身上——这个以举止文雅、政治开明著称(依我看颇为名不副实)的民族。中国商人只要有机会就会行骗,因为反正已被认定不可能诚实行事;中国农民只要不至于被抓就会偷窃,因为惩罚不过是竹板,而他本来天天都可能挨;中国的亲王或宰相,只要自以为不至于被追究,就会掠夺臣民之财,充作私用。对当权者的贪欲唯一的约束,是来自"可能被查出"的恐惧;荣誉之心、羞耻之惧与正义之感,在大多数居官者身上似乎一概阙如。

紧接第一段之后,是同一论断的后果面。罗素 1922 年所列三宗恶德中的**贪婪**(avarice),锚点就在此处。

## 九、p. 355 文明判词:琐事则大,大事则小

> I think, upon the whole, it may fairly be concluded, that the Chinese have been among the first nations, now existing in the world, to arrive at a certain pitch of perfection, where, from the policy of the government, or some other cause, they have remained stationary: that they were civilized, fully to the same extent they now are, more than two thousand years ago, at a period when all Europe might be considered, comparatively, as barbarous; but that they have since made little progress in any thing, and been retrograde in many things: that, at this moment, compared with Europe, they can only be said to be great in trifles, whilst they are really trifling in every thing that is great.

——〔译〕我想总起来看,可以公允地断定:中国人是现今世上最早达到某种完备程度的民族之一,而自那以后,或因政府的政策,或因别的缘故,他们就停在那里不动了;两千多年前他们的文明程度,已与今日相同,而那时全欧洲相形之下还可算是野蛮的;但此后他们在任何事上都少有进步,在许多事上还退了回去;到今天,与欧洲相比,只能说他们在琐屑之事上伟大,而在一切伟大之事上实在琐屑。

可与 **p. 384** 的中俄对比并读:

> One century ago Russia was but just emerging from a state of barbarism, and in a century hence, in all human probability, she will make a conspicuous figure among European nations, both in arts and arms. Two thousand years ago China was civilized to the same degree, or nearly so, that she is at present. The governments were both arbitrary, and the people were slaves. The one is in a state of youthful vigour, advancing daily in strength and knowledge; the other is worn out with old age and disease, and under its present state of existence is not likely to advance in any kind of improvement.

——〔译〕一百年前,俄国才刚从野蛮状态中脱出;而再过一百年,照人事之常理推想,她将在技艺与武力两方面都在欧洲各国中居于显赫之列。两千年前的中国,文明程度已与今日相同或相去不远。两国的政府都是专断的,两国的人民都是奴隶。然而一个正处在少壮之年的旺盛里,日日在力量与知识上前进;另一个已被老病耗尽,以其现有的存在方式,看来不可能在任何方面有所长进。

注意这段的分类法:巴罗把中国与俄国归入**同一类**(政府专断、人民为奴),差别只在少壮与衰老。可见他讲的是专制政体的通病,不是中国独有的病。

## 十、p. 176 对照组:南洋华人(全书最有力的一段)

> These unfavourable features in the character of a people, whose natural disposition is neither ferocious nor morose; but, on the contrary, mild, obliging, and cheerful, can be attributed only to the habits in which they have been trained, and to the heavy hand of power perpetually hanging over them. That this is actually the case may be inferred from the general conduct and character of those vast multitudes who, from time to time, have emigrated to the Philippine islands, Batavia, Pulo Pinang and other parts of our East Indian settlements. In those places they are not less remarkable for their honesty, than for their peaceable and industrious habits.

——〔译〕这个民族性格中的种种不良之处——而他们天生的性情既不凶暴也不阴郁,恰恰相反,是温和、乐于助人而开朗的——只能归因于他们被训练成的那些习惯,以及那只永远悬在他们头顶的、沉重的权力之手。事实确实如此,这可以从那些历年移居菲律宾群岛、巴达维亚、槟榔屿以及我国东印度其他属地的大量华人的一般行为与品性推断出来:在那些地方,他们之以诚实著称,不亚于他们之以平和勤勉著称。

同样的人,换掉压在头上的东西,一代之内就是另一副样子。巴罗自己给出了这个对照组,并且明确写下 **trained** 与 **the heavy hand of power**——与达尔文写巴西那个黑人所用的词完全相同。

前九段若无第十段,便只是一份罪状;有了第十段,它才成为一个论证。

**附带一段值得同读:p. 383 俄国的笞责**

> A similar kind of personal chastisement for light offences, or misconduct, was inflicted in Russia on persons of all ranks, but with this difference, that the correction was private and by order of the Sovereign alone. The Czar Peter, indeed, generally bestowed a drubbing on his courtiers with his own hand; who, instead of being dishonoured or disgraced by such a castigation, were supposed, from that very circumstance, to be his...

——〔译〕在俄国,轻罪或失职也有类似的体罚,加于各等人身上,不同之处在于:施罚是私下进行的,且只由君主一人下令。彼得大帝更是常常亲手打他的廷臣一顿;而受此鞭挞者非但不以为辱、不以为耻,反倒因此被视为是他的〔亲信〕……

可见"受责谢恩"并非中国独有。清朝的独特之处在于把它**制度化、公开化,并普及到除皇帝以外的每一个人**。

# 第二部分 巴罗《中国行纪》段落汇编(全录)

本部分按原书章次排列,包含总纲十段在内的全部段落,便于查检上下文。

## 第一章 前言:使团与传教士记述之辨

- **【艺术贫乏】**(p. 4–5)
  > But the expectations of the man of science, the artist, or the naturalist, might perhaps be rather disappointed, than their curiosity be gratified, in travelling through this extensive country. It can boast of few works of art, few remains of ancient grandeur. The great wall, that for a time defended its peaceable inhabitants against the attacks of the roving Tartars, the walls of its numerous cities, with their square towers and lofty gates, and here and there an old pagoda, are its only architectural antiquities; and, when these are excepted, there is not perhaps a single building in the whole extent of China that has withstood the action of three centuries.
  ——〔译〕科学家、艺术家或博物学家游历这片广袤国土,恐怕失望多于满足。此地少有艺术杰作,也少有昔日宏伟的遗存。长城曾一度护卫其安分的居民抵御游荡的韃靼人,此外便只有众多城池的城墙、方形角楼与高大城门,以及零星几座古塔——这就是它全部的建筑古迹。除此之外,全中国大概找不出一座经得住三百年岁月的建筑。

- **【无古迹可观】**(p. 5)
  > In vain should we here look for the massy and stupendous fabrics that appear in the pyramids and the pillars of the ancient Egyptians; the beautiful and symmetrical works of art displayed in the temples of the Greeks; the grand and magnificent remains of Roman architecture; or that combination of convenience and elegance of design which characterize the modern buildings of Europe.
  ——〔译〕在这里寻找古埃及金字塔与石柱那般厚重惊人的建构,是徒劳的;希腊神庙中那种匀称优美的艺术,罗马建筑那种宏伟壮丽的遗迹,以及近代欧洲建筑所特有的实用与雅致兼备的设计,这里都找不到。

- **【千城一面】**(p. 5–6)
  > In China every city is nearly the same: a quadrangular space of ground is enclosed with walls of stone, of brick, or of earth, all built upon the same plan; the houses within them of the same construction; and the streets, except the principal ones that run from gate to gate, invariably narrow. The temples are, nearly, all alike, of the same awkward design as the dwelling-houses, but on a larger scale... The manners, the dress, the amusements of the people, are nearly the same.
  ——〔译〕中国的城市几乎千篇一律:一片四方地界,以石、砖或土筑墙围起,布局全都一个样;城内房屋结构相同;除了由城门通向城门的几条主街,街巷一概狭窄。庙宇也几乎全无分别,与民居同样笨拙的式样,只是规模更大……百姓的举止、衣着与娱乐,也大同小异。

- **【剥去粉饰】**(p. 3–4)
  > the chief aim of which will be to shew this extraordinary people in their proper colours, not as their own moral maxims would represent them, but as they really are—to divest the court of the tinsel and the tawdry varnish with which, like the palaces of the Emperor, the missionaries have found it expedient to cover it in their writings.
  ——〔译〕本书首要之旨,是让这个奇特的民族显出本色:不照他们自己的道德格言所描绘的样子,而照他们实际的样子——把宫廷身上那层俗艳的金箔与浮华的漆彩剥去,那正是传教士在著述中觉得便于涂抹上去的,一如他们涂饰皇帝的宫殿。

- **【文明位次】**(p. 4)
  > as may enable the reader to settle, in his own mind, the point of rank which China may be considered to hold in the scale of civilized nations.
  ——〔译〕以便读者能够自行判定,在文明各国的序列中,中国应当置于何等位次。

- **【荷使受辱】**(p. 9–10)
  > these two Embassadors... cheerfully submitted to every humiliating ceremony required from them by the Chinese, who, in return, treated them in the most contemptuous and indignant manner.
  ——〔译〕这两位使臣……欣然屈从了中国人要求他们行的每一项屈辱礼节;而中国人的回报,则是以极其轻蔑无礼的态度对待他们。

- **【叩头三十次】**(p. 10–11)
  > At Pekin they were required to humiliate themselves at least thirty different times, at each of which they were obliged, on their knees, to knock their heads nine times against the ground, which Mr. Van Braam, in his journal, very coolly calls, performing the salute of honour, "faire le salut d'honneur." And they were finally dismissed, with a few paltry pieces of silk, without having once been allowed to open their lips on any kind of business... On their arrival in this Capital they were lodged, literally, in a stable; under the same cover, and in the same apartment, with a parcel of cart-horses.
  ——〔译〕在北京,他们至少有三十次被要求自取其辱,每一次都须跪地以头触地九下;而范罷覽先生在日记里竟从容地称之为行"荣誉之礼"("faire le salut d'honneur")。最后他们被打发走,只得了几匹不值钱的绸缎,而通商事务连一个字都不许提……初到京城时,他们被安置在一处马厩里——一点不夸张,与一群拉车的马同处一檩之下、同居一室。

- **【卑顺助其骄】**(p. 11)
  > After such a vile reception and degrading treatment of the Dutch Embassy, what advantages can reasonably be expected to accrue from a servile and unconditional compliance with the submissions required by this haughty government? It would rather seem that their exactions are proportioned to the complying temper of the persons with whom they have to treat.
  ——〔译〕荷兰使团既遭此等下贱的接待与屈辱的对待,那么对这个傲慢政府所要求的屈从奴颜婢膝、无条件应承,还能指望得到什么好处?情形倒像是:他们的索求,正与所交涉之人的顺从程度成正比。

- **【固执旧章】**(p. 20–21)
  > such is the pride and the haughty insolence of the Chinese government, that, in no instance on record, but that of the British embassy, has it ever relaxed from its long established customs, nor acquiesced in any demands of foreign embassadors... The forms of the court they contend to be as immutable as were the laws of the Medes and Persians.
  ——〔译〕中国政府之骄慢无礼一至于此:有案可稽者中,除英国使团一例,它从未在自己积久的成规上让过一步,也从未应允外国使臣的任何要求……他们坚称宫廷礼制不可更易,如同米底亚人与波斯人的法律一般。

- **【顺从只助其骄】**(p. 24)
  > a tone of submission, and a tame and passive obedience to the degrading demands of this haughty court, serve only to feed its pride, and add to the absurd notions of its own vast importance.
  ——〔译〕对这个傲慢朝廷的屈辱要求取屈从的口气、作驯顺被动的听命,只会滋养它的骄气,更助长它那种自以为举足轻重的荒谬念头。

## 第二章 航海与初入中国

- **【停滞不进】**(p. 29)
  > That China was civilized to a certain degree before most of the nations of Europe, not even Greece excepted, is a fact that will not admit of a doubt; but that it has continued to improve, so as still to vie with many of the present European states, as the missionaries would have it supposed, is not by any means so clear. From the middle to the end of the sixteenth century, compared with Europe in general, it had greatly the superiority... The Chinese were, at that period, pretty much in the same state in which they still are; and in which they are likely to continue.
  ——〔译〕中国在欧洲多数民族(连希腊亦不例外)之前即已达到某种程度的文明,这一点毋庸置疑;但如传教士所愿人们相信的那样,说它持续进步、至今仍可与今日欧洲诸国抗衡,则远不明白。十六世纪中叶至末叶,与整个欧洲相比,它确实大占优势……而那时的中国人,大致就是今日的样子;并且看来还将一直如此。

- **【传教士自相矛盾】**(p. 31)
  > In the same breath that they extol the wonderful strength of filial piety, they speak of the common practices of exposing infants; the strict morality and ceremonious conduct of the people are followed by a list of the most gross debaucheries; the virtues and the philosophy of the learned are explained by their ignorance and their vices; if in one page they speak of the excessive fertility of the country, and the amazing extension of agriculture, in the next, thousands are seen perishing by want; and whilst they extol with admiration the progress they have made in the arts and sciences, they plainly inform us that without the aid of foreigners they can neither cast a cannon, nor calculate an eclipse.
  ——〔译〕他们一面盛赞孝道之力何等惊人,一面又说弃婴之事习以为常;刚讲民风严谨、礼节周备,接着便列出种种最粗鄙的淫纵;学者的德行与哲思,随即由其愚昧与恶习来注释;前一页说土地肥沃无极、农耕推广可惊,后一页便见成千人饿死;正当他们赞叹中国人在技艺与学术上的进步时,又明白告诉我们:没有外人相助,他们既不能铸一门炮,也算不出一次日食。

- **【浮夸虚数】**(p. 36)
  > when a Chinese made use of the monosyllable van, which in his language signifies ten thousand, he was not to be understood as speaking of a determinate or precise number, but only as making use of a term that implied amplification. A state criminal, for example, is generally condemned to undergo the punishment of being cut into ten thousand pieces; the great wall of China is called the van-lee-tchin, or wall of ten thousand lee... a length just double to that which the most authentic accounts have given of it.
  ——〔译〕中国人说出"万"这个单音字时,不可当作确定精确的数目,只是一个表示夸大的字眼而已。譬如国事重犯,照例判处"万段"之刑;中国的长城称为"万里城",即万里之墙……这个长度正好是最可靠记载所载的两倍。

- **【航海无术】**(p. 38)
  > The Chinese, in fact, are equally unskilled in naval architecture, as in the art of navigation. They keep no reckoning at sea, nor possess the least idea of drawing imaginary lines upon the surface of the globe... they have no means whatsoever of ascertaining the latitude or the longitude of any place, either by estimation from the distance sailed, or by observation of the heavenly bodies, with instruments for that purpose.
  ——〔译〕中国人于造船一术与航海一术,同样拙劣。他们在海上不作推算,对在地球表面画出假想经纬线一事毫无概念……无论凭航程推测,还是用仪器观测天体,他们都绝无办法测定任何地点的纬度或经度。

- **【天圆地方中国居中】**(p. 39)
  > it being among them, in every period of their history, an universally received opinion, that the earth is a square, and that the kingdom of China is placed in the very center of its flat surface.
  ——〔译〕在他们历史上的每一个时期,都普遍持有这样一种见解:大地是方的,而中国就坐落在这块平面的正中央。

- **【官吏支吾其词】**(p. 57)
  > To every question that led to the main point of our visit, these people gave us evasive answers, affecting the most complete ignorance of every thing relating to the affairs of the embassy.
  ——〔译〕凡问到与我们此行要旨相关之处,这些人一概支吾以对,装作对使团事务一无所知。

- **【专制拉夫】**(p. 59)
  > This arbitrary proceeding of the Governor conveyed no very exalted ideas of the justice or moderation of the government, or of the protection it afforded to the subject. To drag away from his family an honest and industrious citizen, settled in trade, and to force him into a service that must be ruinous to his concerns, was an act of injustice and violence that could not be tolerated in any other than a despotic government, where the subject knows no laws but the will of the tyrant.
  ——〔译〕这位总督的专断行径,使人对该政府的公正与宽和、对它给臣民的保护,都难有高明的评价。把一个安分勤勉、已有生业的市民从家中拖走,强迫他去服一项必将毁其营生的差役,这种不义与暴虐,除了专制政体之外,任何政体都不能容忍;在那里,臣民所知的法律只有暴君的意志。

- **【官长之无情】**(p. 60)
  > The old Governor was evidently relieved from a load of anxiety at his success; and the tears and entreaties of the poor men served only to brighten up his countenance.
  ——〔译〕老总督因得手而显然如释重负;那些可怜人的眼泪与哀求,只不过使他的神色更加开朗罢了。

- **【好奇而无求知】**(p. 61)
  > it was that sort of curiosity, which appeared rather to be incited by the desire of looking narrowly at the persons of those who were to have the honour of being presented to their Great Emperor, than for the sake of gratifying the eye or the mind, by the acquirement of information or new ideas... although eager to get a transient glance at the passengers, their curiosity was satisfied in a moment.
  ——〔译〕那是这样一种好奇:似乎只出于想细看一眼即将有幸觐见其大皇帝之人,而非为了增长见闻、获得新知以悦目悦心……他们虽急于对过客瞥上一眼,好奇心却转瞬即足。

- **【贫陋失望】**(p. 71)
  > Every thing, in fact, that we had hitherto seen wore an air of poverty and meanness... on our first landing in this celebrated empire to the present place, which is no great distance from the capital, I am persuaded, that every individual of the embassy felt himself rather disappointed in the expectations he had formed.
  ——〔译〕其实我们迄今所见的一切,都带着一股贫穷与鄙陋之气……自初登这个著名帝国之岸,直到此处——离京城已不甚远——我确信使团中每一个人都觉得自己原先的期望颇为落空。

- **【缠足之残忍】**(p. 73)
  > The constant pain and uneasiness that female children must necessarily suffer, in the act of compressing, by means of bandages, the toes under the sole of the foot, and retaining them in that position until they literally grow into and become a part of it; and by forcing the heel forward, until it is entirely obliterated, make it the more wonderful how a custom, so unnatural and inhuman, should have continued for so many ages.
  ——〔译〕女童必受的那种长久痛苦与不适——用布帛把脚趾压到脚掌之下,一直保持到它们真的长进脚掌、成为其一部分;又把脚跟向前逼挤,直至完全消失——愈使人惊异:如此违反天性、如此不近人情的风俗,竟能延续如此多世代。

- **【男制其女】**(p. 76)
  > the Chinese must be allowed to be well versed in the management of the sex, to have so far gained the ascendancy over them, as to prevail upon them to adopt a fashion, which required a voluntary relinquishment of one of the greatest pleasures and blessings of life, the faculty of locomotion; and to contrive to render this fashion so universal that any deviation from it should be considered as disgraceful.
  ——〔译〕必须承认中国人深谙驾驭女性之术:他们对女子的压制竟到如此地步,能诱使她们采纳一种时尚,自愿放弃人生最大的快乐与福分之一——行动的能力;并且设法使这种时尚如此普遍,以致稍有违背便被视为耻辱。

- **【污秽不洁】**(p. 77)
  > This, indeed, forms no part of their character; on the contrary they are what Swift would call a frowzy people. The comfort of clean linen, or frequent change of under-garments, is equally unknown to the Sovereign and to the peasant... The highest officers of state made no hesitation of calling their attendants in public to seek in their necks for those troublesome animals, which, when caught, they very composedly put between their teeth.
  ——〔译〕整洁绝非他们性格的一部分;恰恰相反,他们正是斯威夫特所说的"腌臢"之民。洁净衣衬之舒适、内衣勤换之习惯,君主与农夫同样一无所知……最高的朝廷大员毫不迟疑地当众唤来随从,在自己颈间搜捉那些讨厌的小虫;捉到之后,便从容不迫地放进牙齿间咬死。

- **【国政改易天性】**(p. 82)
  > In short, had we returned to Europe, without proceeding farther in the country than Tien-sing, a most lively impression would always have remained on my mind in favour of the Chinese. But a variety of incidents that afterwards occurred, and a more intimate acquaintance with their manners and habits, produced a woeful change of sentiment in this respect... to which, and to the nature of the executive government, all their moral actions may be referred: and by the influence of which, the natural bent of their character evidently has undergone a complete change.
  ——〔译〕总之,倘使我们只到天津便返回欧洲,我心中对中国人必将永远留着极鲜明的好感。但此后发生的种种事故,以及对其风俗习惯更亲切的了解,使这份情感发生了可悲的转变……他们一切道德行为,都可归因于此,并归因于其行政统治之性质;在这种影响之下,他们性格的天然倾向显然已完全改变。

## 第三章 北京与圆明园

- **【一声令下万人役】**(p. 88)
  > Every thing here, in fact, seems to be at the instant command of the state; and the most laborious tasks are undertaken and executed with a readiness, and even a chearfulness, which one could scarcely expect to meet with in so despotic a government.
  ——〔译〕此地万事似乎都听凭国家一声令下即可办成;最繁重的苦役也照样有人承接、迅速完成,且干得颇为爽快——这在如此专制的政体之下,原是不大指望能见到的。

- **【京城之陋】**(p. 93)
  > The first appearance of this celebrated capital is not much calculated to raise high expectations, nor does it in the least improve upon a more intimate acquaintance... In Pekin not even a chimney is seen rising above the roofs of the houses which, being all nearly of the same height, and the streets laid out in straight lines, have the appearance and the regularity of a large encampment.
  ——〔译〕这座名闻天下的京城,初看便不足以令人生出多高的期待,待熟识之后也丝毫没有改观。北京城里连一根烟囱都不见高出屋顶;屋舍高矮几乎一律,街道又全是笔直排开,整座城看去就像一座规整的大兵营。

- **【妇女幽禁】**(p. 98)
  > The Chinese women are more scrupulously confined to the house in the capital than elsewhere. Young girls were sometimes seen smoking their pipes in the doors of their houses, but they always retired on the approach of men.
  ——〔译〕中国妇女深居闺门,在京城比别处管束得更严。偶尔可见年轻女子在自家门口吸烟袋,但一见男子走近,便立刻退入门内。

- **【秽气终日】**(p. 99)
  > Thus, though the city is cleared of its filth, it seldom loses its fragrance. In fact, a constant disgusting odour remains in and about all the houses the whole day long, from the fermentation of the heterogeneous mixtures kept above ground, which in our great cities are carried off in drains.
  ——〔译〕于是,城中的污物虽被清走,那股"香气"却难得散去。各家屋内屋外终日弥漫着一种令人作恶的臭味,盖因各色秽物都堆在地面上发酵——这类东西在我国的大城市里是由下水沟排走的。

- **【建筑无美】**(p. 101)
  > He will immediately perceive that every street is laid out in the same manner, and every house built upon the same plan; and that their architecture is void of taste, grandeur, beauty, solidity, or convenience; that the houses are merely tents, and that there is nothing magnificent, even in the palace of the Emperor... All, however, is not gold that glitters in China, more than elsewhere.
  ——〔译〕他一眼便看出:条条街道布置雷同,家家房屋出于一个式样;其建筑既无品味,又无气象,更谈不上美观、坚固与适用;所谓房屋不过是帐篷而已,即便皇帝的宫殿也无一处堪称壮丽。总之在中国,和在别处一样,闪光的并非都是金子。

- **【暗探随行】**(p. 106)
  > so suspicious were the officers of government of any communication with these gentlemen that they were invariably accompanied by some of them to act as spies, notwithstanding they could not comprehend one single word that was exchanged in the conversations they held together... But he watches the actions, and even the motions of the eye, and makes his report accordingly.
  ——〔译〕官府对任何人与这几位先生往来都疑心重重,以至每次会面必派官员随行充作耳目,尽管他们对交谈的言语一个字也听不懂。可他仍要留意举动,连眼神的转动也不放过,而后据此具报。

- **【文字阻碍学术】**(p. 106)
  > The length of time that is usually required by the Chinese, together with the intense study and stretch of the memory which they find necessary in order to obtain a very small proportion of the characters that form the language, are serious obstructions to the progress of the arts and sciences, but favourable to the stability of the government of which indeed the language may be considered as one of the great bulwarks.
  ——〔译〕中国人即便只想掌握本国文字中极小的一部分,也须耗费漫长岁月、苦读不辍并极度劳役记忆。此事大为妨碍技艺与科学的进步,却有利于政权的稳固——其文字实可算是这政权的一大屏障。

- **【大臣居所之陋】**(p. 109)
  > The apartments were somewhat larger, but miserably dirty both within and without, and wholly unfurnished... If the menial servants of his Britannic Majesty's Ministers were no better lodged than the ministers themselves of his Chinese Majesty, they would be apt to think themselves very ill used.
  ——〔译〕房间稍大一些,但内外都脏得不堪,且全无家具陈设。倘若我大不列颠国王大臣家中的下等仆役,住处也不过如中国皇帝的大臣本人这般,他们准会觉得自己受了极大的委屈。

- **【钦天监之无知】**(p. 111)
  > It was obvious, from the few questions put by the president of this learned body, that he had conceived the planetarium to be something similar to one of those curious pieces of musical mechanism which, in the Canton jargon, are called Sing-songs, and that nothing more was necessary than to wind it up like a jack, when it would immediately spin round, and tell him every thing that he wanted to know.
  ——〔译〕从这个博学衙门的主官所提的那几个问题看,显然他把天体仪当成了广东话里叫作"Sing-song"(响器)的那类希奇的音乐机械,以为只要像给烤肉转叉上弦一样拧紧发条,它便立刻转动起来,把他想知道的一切都告诉他。

- **【历法实抄巴黎】**(p. 112)
  > He candidly avowed that neither he nor any of his European brethren were well qualified for the task, and that they had been hitherto more indebted to the Connoissances de tems of Paris than to their own calculations. That having exactly ascertained the difference of meridians between Pekin and Paris, they had little difficulty in reducing the calculations made for the latter, so as to answer for the situation of the former... not to be detected by any of the Chinese members.
  ——〔译〕他坦然承认,他与欧洲同侪都不足以胜任此事,历来所倚仗的多是巴黎《时间知识》天文年历,而非自家的推算。既已精确测定北京与巴黎两地子午线之差,他们只需把为巴黎所作的推算折算一番,便可合用于北京,几乎毫不费力——而钦天监的中国官员中无一人能察觉此中蹊跷。

- **【视英为夷】**(p. 118)
  > the records of the country might hand it down to posterity, as an event that had tarnished the lustre of his reign, being nothing short of breaking through an ancient custom, and adopting one of a barbarous nation in its place.
  ——〔译〕本朝史册或将此事传于后世,视为有损其治世光辉的一桩污点,因为这无异于破坏古老成法,而以一个蛮夷之国的礼节取而代之。

- **【宫殿不如英仓】**(p. 124)
  > These assemblages of buildings, which they dignify with the name of palaces, are, however, of such a nature as to be more remarkable for their number than for their splendour or magnificence. A great proportion of the buildings consists in mean cottages. The very dwelling of the Emperor and the grand hall in which he gives audience, when divested of the gilding and the gaudy colours with which they are daubed, are little superior, and much less solid, than the barns of a substantial English farmer.
  ——〔译〕这些被他们尊称为"宫殿"的建筑群,其可观之处在数目之多,而不在辉煌壮丽。其中大部分只是简陋的屋舍。就连皇帝本人的寝居和接见臣属的大殿,若剥去那涂抹其上的金饰与花俏的彩色,也比不上一个殷实的英国农夫的谷仓——论坚固更是远远不及。

## 第四章 中国的社会状况(全书最集中的一章)

- **【妇女地位卑贱】**(p. 141)
  > The Chinese, if possible, have imposed on their women a greater degree of humility and restraint than the Greeks of old, or the Europeans in the dark ages. Not satisfied with the physical deprivation of the use of their limbs, they have contrived, in order to keep them the more confined, to make it a moral crime for a woman to be seen abroad.
  ——〔译〕中国人加在妇女身上的卑屈与拘束,恐怕比古希腊人、比黑暗时代的欧洲人更甚。他们不满足于从肉体上剥夺妇女行动的能力,还要进一步把她禁闭起来:凡妇人露面于外,便算作一种道德上的罪过。

- **【家庭情感之扼杀】**(p. 142)
  > Even at home, in her own family, a woman must neither eat at the same table, nor sit in the same room with her husband. And the male children, at the age of nine or ten, are entirely separated from their sisters. Thus the feelings of affection... are nipped in the very bud of dawning sentiment. A cold and ceremonious conduct must be observed on all occasions between the members of the same family. There is no common focus to attract and concentrate the love and respect of children for their parents.
  ——〔译〕即便在家中、在自己的家族里,妇人也不得与丈夫同桌而食、同室而坐;男孩到了九岁十岁,便与姐妹完全隔离。于是种种亲爱之情,在刚刚萌芽之际便被掐断。同一家人之间,无论何时都须守着冷淡而拘礼的举止。子女对父母的爱与敬,没有一个共同的焦点可以凝聚。

- **【少年老成无生气】**(p. 143)
  > the stiff and ceremonious behaviour, which constitutes no inconsiderable part of their education, throws a restraint on all the little playful actions incident to their time of life and completely subdues all spirit of activity and enterprize. A Chinese youth of the higher class is inanimate, formal, and inactive, constantly endeavouring to assume the gravity of years.
  ——〔译〕那种僵硬而拘礼的举止,在他们的教育中占了不小的分量,把这个年纪本该有的一切嬉戏之举都束缚住了,把活泼进取之气也彻底压服。一个上层人家的中国少年,毫无生气,一味守礼,不肯动作,时时刻刻都在勉力装出老年人的庄重。

- **【孝为法令非情感】**(p. 144)
  > Filial duty is, in fact, in China, less a moral sentiment, than a precept which by length of time has acquired the efficacy of a positive law; and it may truly be said to exist more in the maxims of the government, than in the minds of the people.
  ——〔译〕在中国,孝道其实并非一种道德情感,而是一条年深日久便取得了成文法效力的训条;可以说它存在于政府的箴规之中,多于存在于百姓的心中。

- **【婚姻买卖无爱】**(p. 146)
  > To what a degraded condition is a female reduced by this absurd custom!... In a word, that state of the human heart, occasioned by the mutual affection between the sexes, and from whence proceed the happiest, the most interesting, and sometimes also, the most distressing moments of life, has no existence in China. The man takes a wife because the laws of the country direct him to do so, and custom has made it indispensable; and the woman, after marriage, continues to be the same piece of inanimate furniture she always was in her father's house.
  ——〔译〕这种荒谬的习俗把女子贬到了何等卑下的地位!总而言之,那种由两性相悦而生的心境——人生中最快乐、最动人、有时也最痛苦的时刻皆由此而来——在中国并不存在。男子娶妻,只因国法如此规定、习俗使之不可免;而女子出嫁之后,仍旧是她在父家时那件毫无生气的家具。

- **【父暴子奴无社交】**(p. 152)
  > A tyrant, in fact, to command, and a slave to obey, are found in every family; for, where the father is a despot, the son will naturally be a slave; and if all the little acts of kindness and silent attentions, that create mutual endearments, be wanting among the members of the same family, living under the same roof, it will be in vain to expect to find them in the enlarged sphere of public life. In fact, they have no kind of friendly societies nor meetings to talk over the transactions and the news of the day. These can only take place in a free government.
  ——〔译〕每一个家庭里都有一个施令的暴君和一个听命的奴隶:父既专制,子自然为奴。倘同一屋檐下的家人之间,连那些生出相互亲爱之情的小小善意与默默关切都没有,那么在更广的公共生活里也就休想找到。事实上他们既无friendly societies一类的团体,也没有聚会来谈论时事与新闻——这些唯有在自由的政体之下才能出现。

- **【宴无欢洽】**(p. 155)
  > But even in those feasts there is nothing that bears the resemblance of conviviality... their eyes must constantly be kept upon the master of the feast, to watch all his motions, and to observe every morsel he puts into his mouth.
  ——〔译〕即便在这些宴席上,也没有一丝一毫近于欢洽之乐的东西:众人的眼睛须时时盯着主人,察看他的一举一动,留意他送入口中的每一口食物。

- **【赌风质妻鬻子】**(p. 159)
  > The spirit of gaming is so universal in most of the towns and cities, that in almost every bye-corner, groupes are to be found playing at cards or throwing dice. They are accused even of frequently staking their wives and children on the hazard of a die.
  ——〔译〕赌风在多数城镇之中如此普遍,几乎每个僻巷角落都可见成群的人在打牌掷骰。更有人指控他们常把妻子儿女押在一掷之间。

- **【法制败坏天性(核心段)】**(p. 160)
  > I have already had occasion to observe that the natural disposition of the Chinese should seem to have suffered almost a total change by the influence of the laws and maxims of government, an influence which, in this country more than elsewhere, has given a bias to the manners, sentiments, and moral character of the people; for here every ancient proverb carries with it the force of a law. While they are by nature quiet, passive, and timid, the state of society and the abuse of the laws by which they are governed, have rendered them indifferent, unfeeling, and even cruel, as a few examples, which among many others occurred, will but too clearly bear evidence.
  ——〔译〕我此前已有机会指出:中国人的天性,似乎在法律与政府箴规的影响之下几乎全然变了样。这种影响在此国比在别处更甚,它使国民的举止、情感与道德品格都发生了偏斜;因为在这里,每一句古老的谚语都带有法律的效力。他们生性本是安静、被动而怯懦的,然而其社会状况以及统治他们的法律之被滥用,却使他们变得漠然、无情,甚至残忍——我所遇见的诸多事例之中,只举数例,便足以明明白白地作证。

- **【笞刑之滥】**(p. 161)
  > However lightly these gentlemen may chuse to treat this humiliating chastisement, to which all are liable from the prime minister to the peasant, it is but too often inflicted in the anger and by the caprice of a man in office, and frequently with circumstances of unwarrantable cruelty and injustice.
  ——〔译〕这些先生们尽可把这种屈辱的刑罚说得轻描淡写——上自宰相下至农夫无人可免——但它实在太常出于某个当官者一时之怒、一时之兴,而且往往伴随着毫无道理的残酷与不公。

- **【见溺不救】**(p. 167)
  > Although numbers of boats were sailing about the place, none were perceived to go to the assistance of those that were struggling in the water. They even seemed not to know that such an accident had happened, nor could the shrieks of the boys, floating on pieces of the wreck, attract their attention. One fellow was observed very busily employed in picking up, with his boat-hook, the hat of a drowning man.
  ——〔译〕虽有许多船只在近处往来,却不见一只前去救援那些在水中挣扎的人。他们甚至似乎不知道出了这样的事故,连抱着船板浮在水面的男孩的尖叫也引不起他们的注意。只见一个家伙很忙碌地用船钩去捞一个溺水者的帽子。

- **【溺婴为冷酷之证】**(p. 168)
  > Being thus insensible to the sufferings of their companions and countrymen, little compassion is to be expected from them towards strangers. But, if further proofs were wanting to establish the insensible and incompassionate character of the Chinese, the horrid practice of infanticide, tolerated by custom and encouraged by the government, can leave no doubt on this subject.
  ——〔译〕他们对同伴与同国人的苦难既如此无感,对外人自然更谈不上有什么恻隐之心。倘若还需要更多的证据来确定中国人麻木无情的性格,那么那种为习俗所容、为政府所纵的可怕的溺婴之风,便足以使此事无可置疑。

- **【婴尸浮江无人顾】**(p. 171)
  > This hazardous experiment, in a country where humanity appears to be reduced to so low an ebb, can only be considered as an aggravation of cruelty. I have seen the dead body of an infant, but without any gourd, floating down the river of Canton among the boats, and the people seemed to take no more notice of it than if it had been the carcase of a dog.
  ——〔译〕在一个人道似已低落至此的国度里,这种拿性命作赌的办法只能算是残酷之上再添残酷。我曾见一具婴儿的尸体,身上并无葫芦,在广州江上的船只之间顺流而下,而众人视之,竟不比一条狗的尸首更加在意。

- **【人心麻木】**(p. 174)
  > so many examples daily occur of inhumanity, of summary punishments, acts of violence and cruelty, that the mind becomes callous and habituated to scenes that once would have shocked, and is at length scarcely susceptible of the enormity of crimes.
  ——〔译〕不仁之事、不经审理的刑罚、暴虐残忍之举,每日层出不穷,以致人心变得坚硬,对本应令人震骇的情景习以为常,终至对罪恶之大几乎再无感觉。

- **【孝道之虚伪】**(p. 175)
  > How very weak then, in reality, must be the boasted filial affection of the Chinese for their parents, when they scruple not to become the murderers of their own children.
  ——〔译〕既然他们杀害自己的子女都毫不迟疑,那么中国人所夸耀的对父母的孝爱,实际上该是何等薄弱。

- **【海外华人反诚实】**(p. 176)
  > These unfavourable features in the character of a people, whose natural disposition is neither ferocious nor morose; but, on the contrary, mild, obliging, and cheerful, can be attributed only to the habits in which they have been trained, and to the heavy hand of power perpetually hanging over them. That this is actually the case may be inferred from the general conduct and character of those vast multitudes who, from time to time, have emigrated to the Philippine islands, Batavia, Pulo Pinang and other parts of our East Indian settlements. In those places they are not less remarkable for their honesty, than for their peaceable and industrious habits.
  ——〔译〕这个民族的天性既不凶暴也不阴郁,恰恰相反,是温和、肯帮忙而快活的;那么他们性格中这些不良的特征,只能归因于他们所受的习染,以及那只永远高悬在他们头上的权力之重手。此说确实与否,可从历来大批移居菲律宾群岛、巴达维亚、槟榔屿以及我国东印度各属地的华人的一般行为与品性推知:在那些地方,他们之诚实与其安分勤劳一样地为人所称。

- **【礼有文而无诚】**(p. 177)
  > The exterior deportment of every class in China is uncommonly decent, and all their manners mild and engaging... hence it follows that they are ceremonious without sincerity, studious of the forms only of politeness without either the ease or elegance of good-breeding.
  ——〔译〕在中国,各阶层的外表举止都异常得体,他们的仪态一概温和悦人……由此便有这样的结果:他们守礼而无诚意,只讲求礼貌的形式,却既无良好教养的从容,也无其优雅。

- **【怯懦冠于天下】**(p. 178)
  > The act of drawing a sword, or presenting a pistol, is sufficient to frighten a common Chinese into convulsions; and their warriors shew but few symptoms of bravery. The Chinese may certainly be considered among the most timid people on the face of the earth; they seem to possess neither personal courage, nor the least pretence of mind in dangers or difficulties.
  ——〔译〕拔出一把剑、举起一支手枪,就足以把一个普通中国人吓得抽搐;而他们的武人也少有勇武的迹象。中国人确可算作世上最怯懦的民族之一:他们似乎既无个人的胆气,临危遇难时也毫无一点镇定自持可言。

- **【自杀成风】**(p. 178–179)
  > Yet there is perhaps no country where acts of suicide occur more frequently than in China, among the women as well as the men: such acts being marked with no disgrace, are not held in any abhorrence. The government, indeed, should seem to hold out encouragement to suicide, by a very common practice of mitigating the sentence of death, in allowing the criminal to be his own executioner.
  ——〔译〕然而自杀之事恐怕没有哪一国比中国更频繁,男女皆然:此举既不被视为耻辱,也就无人厌恶。政府似乎实在是在鼓励自杀——它有一种极常见的做法,即减轻死刑,准许罪犯自己充当行刑者。

- **【谢恩受杖·人之尊严尽失】**(p. 179)
  > In a government, where every man is liable to be made a slave, where every man is subject to be flogged with the bamboo at the nod of one of the lowest rank of those in office, and where he is compelled to kiss the rod that beats him or, which amounts to the same thing, to thank the tyrant on his knees for the trouble he has taken to correct his morals, high notions of honour and dignified sentiments are not to be expected. Where the maxims of the government commanding, and the opinions of the people agreeing, that corporal punishment may be inflicted, on the ground of a favour conferred upon the person punished, a principle of humiliation is admitted that is well calculated to exclude and obliterate every notion of the dignity of human nature.
  ——〔译〕在这样一种政体之下——人人都可能被弄成奴隶,人人都可能因最低一级官吏的一个颔首而被竹板笞打,并且被迫去亲吻那打他的杖,或者(其实一样)跪下叩谢那暴君费心矫正他的品行——高尚的荣誉观念与有尊严的情感是无从指望的。当政府的箴规如此命令、而民众的意见又予以赞同,认为可以施加体罚、且视之为对受罚者的一种恩典时,这就等于承认了一条屈辱的原则;而这条原则恰足以把关于人性尊严的一切观念排除净尽、抹除无余。

- **【奴隶不受辱·上下交征利】**(p. 179–180)
  > A slave, in fact, cannot be dishonoured. The condition itself of being dependent upon and subject to the caprice of another, without the privilege of appeal, is such a degraded state of the human species, that those who are unfortunately reduced to it have no further ignominy or sense of shame to undergo... A Chinese merchant will cheat, whenever an opportunity offers him the means, because he is considered to be incapable of acting honestly; a Chinese peasant will steal when ever he can do it without danger of being detected, because the punishment is only the bamboo, to which he is daily liable; and a Chinese prince, or a prime minister, will extort the property of the subject, and apply it to his private use, whenever he thinks he can do it with impunity. The only check upon the rapacity of men in power is the influence of fear, arising from the possibility of detection: the love of honour, the dread of shame, and a sense of justice, seem to be equally unfelt by the majority of men in office.
  ——〔译〕奴隶其实是无法被羞辱的。仰人鼻息、听任他人喜怒而又无申诉之权,这种境况本身已是人类如此卑下的状态,以致不幸沦落于此者再没有什么耻辱或羞愧可受了。一个中国商人只要有机可乘便行欺诈,因为人本就认定他不可能诚实行事;一个中国农夫只要不致被拿获便行偷窃,因为刑罚不过是竹板,而竹板他每日都可能挨;一个中国的王公或宰相,只要自以为可以不受惩处,便勒索臣民的财产,拿去供自己私用。对当权者的贪婪,唯一的约束是畏惧——即可能被察觉而生的畏惧;至于爱荣誉、畏羞耻、有正义感,在大多数官员身上似乎一概是感觉不到的。

- **【满汉性情之别】**(p. 186)
  > Those who are better pleased with a blunt sincerity bordering on rudeness than a studied complaisance approaching to servility; who may think it better to be robbed openly than cheated civilly, will be apt to give the preference to the Tartar character.
  ——〔译〕凡是宁取近于粗鲁的直率诚恳、而不喜近于卑屈的刻意奉承者,凡是认为被公然劫掠胜于被客客气气地欺骗者,大概都会更偏爱鞑靼人的性格。

- **【国民性总论】**(p. 187)
  > The general character, however, of the nation is a strange compound of pride and meanness, of affected gravity and real frivolousness, of refined civility and gross indelicacy. With an appearance of great simplicity and openness in conversation, they practise a degree of art and cunning against which an European is but ill prepared.
  ——〔译〕然而这个民族的总体性格,是一种奇怪的混合物:既骄傲又卑劣,既装出庄重又实则轻浮,既有精致的礼貌又有粗鄙的不雅。言谈之间显得极为质朴坦白,实则施展着一种手段与狡诈,欧洲人对此殊无防备。

- **【不知信实为何物】**(p. 188)
  > As a direct refusal to any request would betray a want of good breeding, every proposal finds their immediate acquiescence; they promise without hesitation, but generally disappoint by the invention of some sly pretence or plausible objection. They have no proper sense of the obligations of truth. So little scrupulous indeed are they with regard to veracity, that they will assert and contradict without blushing, as it may best suit the purpose of the moment.
  ——〔译〕由于当面拒绝任何请求会显得没有教养,所以无论你提出什么,他们都立刻应允;许诺毫不迟疑,事后却总是造出些狡猾的借口或看似有理的异议来使你失望。他们对信实的义务毫无恰当的观念。在真话一事上他们实在如此不加顾忌,以致但凡合乎一时之便,便可面不改色地先肯定而后否定。

- **【妄自尊大视人为夷】**(p. 189)
  > The vanity of an usurped national superiority and a high notion of self-importance never forsake them on any occasion. Those advantages in others which they cannot avoid feeling, they will affect not to see. And although they are reduced to the necessity of employing foreigners to regulate their calendar and keep their clocks in order, although they are in the habit of receiving yearly various specimens of art and ingenuity from Europe, yet they pertinaciously affect to consider all the nations of the earth as barbarians in comparison of themselves.
  ——〔译〕那种窃取而来的民族优越感之虚荣,以及自视甚重的高傲观念,无论何时何地都不离他们。凡他人之长处,他们虽不能不感觉到,却装作视而不见。并且,尽管他们不得不雇用外人来厘定历法、修理钟表,尽管他们每年照例要收受来自欧洲的种种工艺巧技之物,他们却仍然执拗地假作认定:与自己相比,地球上一切民族都是蛮夷。

## 第五章 宫廷礼仪与乾隆

- **【宫廷之虚伪(马戛尔尼语)】**(p. 191)
  > "a singular mixture of ostentatious hospitality and inbred suspicion, ceremonious civility and real rudeness, shadowy complaisance and substantial perverseness; and this prevails through all the departments connected with the Court."
  ——〔译〕"这是一种奇特的混合:炫耀式的殷勤待客与根深蒂固的猜忌,礼数周全的客套与实际上的粗鲁,虚有其表的顺从与实实在在的执拗;凡与宫廷有关的各衙门,无一不是如此。"

- **【宫室不及圣詹姆斯】**(p. 194)
  > Were I to compare some of the imperial palaces in China to any royal residence in Europe it would certainly be to Saint James's; but the apartments, the furniture, and conveniences of the latter, bad as they are, infinitely transcend any of those in China.
  ——〔译〕若要把中国的若干皇家宫殿比之于欧洲的哪一处王室居所,那也只能比圣詹姆斯宫;但圣詹姆斯宫的房间、家具与各种便利设施,尽管已属简陋,却仍远远胜过中国的任何宫室。

- **【相府陋于凡尔赛】**(p. 195)
  > In short, the wretched lodgings of the state-officers at the court of Versailles, in the time of the French monarchy, were princely palaces in comparison of those allotted to the first ministers of the Emperor of China.
  ——〔译〕总之,法国王朝时代凡尔赛宫中大臣们那些寒陋的住处,与中国皇帝首辅重臣所分得的居所相比,已可算是王侯的宫殿了。

- **【叩拜如敬神明】**(p. 200)
  > "At length the great band, both vocal and instrumental, struck up with all their powers of harmony, and instantly the whole court fell flat upon their faces before this invisible Nebuchadnezzar. Indeed, in no religion either ancient or modern has the divinity ever been addressed, I believe, with stronger exterior marks of worship and adoration than were this morning paid to the phantom of his Chinese majesty."
  ——〔译〕"终于,歌者与乐师齐奏,极尽和声之力;全朝臣工顿时在这位不露形迹的尼布甲尼撒面前俯身贴地。我确信,古今任何宗教在朝拜神明时,其外在的崇拜与敬畏之状,都不及这一早晨人们献给那位有名无形的中国皇帝的那般强烈。"

- **【宫廷趣味鄙陋】**(p. 207)
  > "However meanly we must think of the taste and delicacy of the court of China, whose most refined amusements seem to be chiefly such as I have now described, together with the wretched dramas of the morning..."
  ——〔译〕"中国宫廷的趣味与教养,实在只能令我们低看:其所谓最高雅的消遣,大抵就是我方才所述的那些,再加上上午那些拙劣不堪的戏剧……"

- **【赐食如饲犬】**(p. 210–211)
  > as a great delicacy from the Emperor's table, were brought in, without any dish, a pair of stag's legs, which the Chinese threw down upon the naked table; and for this mark of imperial favour they were required to make the customary genuflections and nine prostrations.
  ——〔译〕作为御膳所赐的珍馐,送来的是两条鹿腿,连盘子也没有,中国人径直把它们扔在光秃秃的桌面上;而为了这份皇恩,受赐者还须照例屈膝行礼、三跪九叩。
  >
  > "The whole of this disgusting mess was brought upon a dirty plate, and seemed much rather intended to feast a dog than as a refreshment for man. In Holland the meanest beggar would receive in an hospital his allowance in a neater manner; and yet it was intended as a mark of honour on the part of an Emperor towards an Embassador!"
  ——〔译〕"这一堆令人作呕的东西盛在一只脏盘子里送来,看着更像是喂狗的食物,而非款待人的饮食。在荷兰,最下等的乞丐在收容所里所得的一份也比这干净体面;然而这却被当作一位皇帝对一国使臣的礼遇!"

- **【叩头三十次而一无所得】**(p. 217)
  > obliged, at the most unseasonable hours, in the depth of winter... to submit to the degrading ceremony of knocking the head nine times against the ground, at least on thirty different occasions, and without having the satisfaction of gaining by this unconditional compliance any one earthly thing, beyond a compliment from the Emperor, that they went through their prostrations to admiration!
  ——〔译〕在隆冬时节,于最不合时宜的时辰被迫行那屈辱的礼节,以头触地九次,前后至少三十回;而这般无条件的屈从,竟丝毫没有换来任何实际好处,所得者仅是皇帝一句夸奖:说他们叩拜得十分可观!

- **【戏剧淫秽】**(p. 222–223)
  > it is to nature in its most gross, rude, and uncivilized state, ill-agreeing with the boasted morality, high polish, refined delicacy, and ceremonious exterior of the Chinese nation... "that they exist more in state maxims, than in the minds of the people."
  ——〔译〕那所摹写的是最粗鄙、最野蛮、最未开化状态下的人性,与中国人自夸的道德、文雅、精致与礼仪外表极不相称……"可见这些德行更多存在于朝廷的训条之中,而非民众的心里。"
  >
  > The people who encourage them must be sunk very deep in intellectual grossness, and have totally lost sight of all decency. These and similar scenes may be considered among the ill effects of excluding women from their due share of influence in society.
  ——〔译〕助长这类演出的民众,其心智之粗鄙必已沉溺甚深,廉耻之心荡然无存。此类情景,或可视为将妇女排斥于社会应有影响之外所带来的恶果之一。

- **【游艺不如英国乡集】**(p. 224)
  > In short, the greater part of the amusements of the Chinese are, at the present day, of a nature so very puerile, or so gross and vulgar, that the tricks and the puppet-shews which are occasionally exhibited in a common fair of one of the country towns of England, may be considered as comparatively polished, interesting, and rational.
  ——〔译〕总之,今日中国人的娱乐大半幼稚可笑,或粗俗不堪,以至于英国乡镇集市上偶尔上演的杂耍与木偶戏,相比之下反倒显得文雅、有趣而合于理性。

- **【乾隆盛怒踢死其子】**(p. 227)
  > Tchien-Lung... was so shocked and exasperated at the supposed want of filial duty that, in the moment of rage, he gave him a violent kick in an unfortunate place which, after his languishing a few days, proved fatal.
  ——〔译〕乾隆……因认定其子不孝而又惊又怒,盛怒之下猛踢一脚,偏偏踢中要害之处;其子缠绵数日,终告不治。

- **【自以为不死】**(p. 228)
  > "However wild and extravagant," observes Lord Macartney, "such a conceit may be regarded, we know from history how much even the best understandings may be perverted by prosperity."
  ——〔译〕马戛尔尼勋爵评论道:"无论这种自负看来何等荒唐无稽,历史已告诉我们,即便最清明的头脑,也可能被显赫顺遂扭曲到何种程度。"

- **【阉人之恶】**(p. 232)
  > The Chinese eunuchs are addicted to all the vices that distinguish these creatures in other countries... It is difficult to conceive a condition in life more humiliating, or more deplorable, than that of a female slave to an eunuch.
  ——〔译〕中国的阉人沉溺于其他国家同类人所共有的一切恶习……世间境遇之屈辱可悲,恐怕再难想出比给阉人做女奴更甚者。

## 第六章 语言、文学与科学技艺

- **【文字晦涩歧义】**(p. 257)
  > the ambiguity to which they are liable, on account of the frequent figurative expressions and substitution of metaphor for the literal meaning, renders their best compositions extremely obscure... In short, it is a language where much is to be made out that is not expressed.
  ——〔译〕由于频繁使用比喻、以隐喻取代字面本义,其文字极易歧义,以致他们最好的文章也晦涩难解。总之,这是一种大量意思并未说出、而要靠读者自行揣度的语言。

- **【科举阻碍科学】**(p. 263)
  > it will readily occur, that little progress is likely to be made in any of the speculative sciences; and more especially as their assistance is not necessary to obtain the most elevated situations in the government. The examinations to be passed for the attainment of office are principally confined to the knowledge of the language.
  ——〔译〕由此不难想见,任何一门思辨科学都难有进步;尤其是因为这些学问对于攀登政府最高职位毫无用处。求官所须通过的考试,主要限于文字学问一项。

- **【单字离句则无义】**(p. 266)
  > The monosyllabic sound assigned to each charter is applied to so many different meanings, that in its unconnected state it may be said to have no meaning at all.
  ——〔译〕每个字所配的单音节读音,可以承担如此众多互异的意义,以致该字一旦脱离上下文,便可说全无意义。

- **【二千年无进步】**(p. 274)
  > With respect to any branch of polite literature, or speculative science, little improvement seems to have been made in the last two thousand years.
  ——〔译〕就任何一门文雅文学或思辨科学而言,两千年来似乎都不见什么进步。

- **【非停滞乃倒退】**(p. 277)
  > It has been observed that, in this country, the arts, the sciences, and literature, are not progressive; and the five king would lead one to conclude, that they have rather even been retrograde than stationary.
  ——〔译〕人们早已看出,在这个国度,技艺、科学与文学都不是向前发展的;而五经更令人断定:它们不止是停滞,毋宁说是倒退。

- **【天文托于外人】**(p. 284)
  > There is no branch of science which the Chinese affect to value so much, and understand so little, as astronomy... Yet so little progress have they made in this science, that the only part of its functions, which can be called astronomical, has long been committed to the care of foreigners, whom they affect to hold in contempt and to consider as barbarians.
  ——〔译〕没有哪一门学问像天文学这样,被中国人摆出万分看重的样子,而其实所知如此之少。他们在此学上进步之微,以致其职司中唯一真正称得上天文的部分,长期以来都交由外国人掌管——而这些外国人,他们却装作鄙夷,视为蛮夷。

- **【仪器安置全误】**(p. 290)
  > all the observations made with them at Pekin, which is in 39° 55'. north, as well as all those made at Nankin in 32° 4'. north, must have been entirely false: and the very act of placing them so distant from the parallel for which they were constructed, is in itself a sufficient proof of the ignorance of the Chinese in matters of this kind.
  ——〔译〕凡用这些仪器在北纬三十九度五十五分的北京、以及北纬三十二度四分的南京所作的观测,必定全属谬误;而把仪器安置在与其制作所依纬线相去如此之远的地方,这一举动本身,就足以证明中国人在此类事情上的无知。

- **【星占代天文】**(p. 292)
  > I saw, and conversed with, numbers of their learned men at the palace of Yuen-min-yuen, but I can safely say, that not a single Chinese, nor a Tartar, who shewed themselves there, were possessed of the slightest knowledge of astronomy... Astronomy with them consists entirely in a certain jargon of judicial astrology... and they believe the stars to be stuck, like so many nails, at equal distances from the earth, in the blue vault of heaven.
  ——〔译〕我在圆明园见过并交谈过他们许多学者,但我可以断言:在那里露面的中国人与满人当中,没有一个稍具天文学知识。他们的天文学完全是一套占星术的行话;他们相信众星像一枚枚钉子,以相等的距离钉在那片蔚蓝的天穹之上。

- **【几何代数全无】**(p. 296)
  > both the one and the other being totally unknown in the country. Their arithmetic is mechanical.
  ——〔译〕二者在这个国家都全然无人知晓。他们的算术纯属机械操作。

- **【化学不成其为学】**(p. 298)
  > which cannot here be said to exist as a science, although several branches are in common practice as chemical arts... having ascertained the fact, they have given themselves no further trouble to explain the phenomenon.
  ——〔译〕在这里,化学不能说是作为一门科学而存在,尽管其若干分支作为化学工艺已在通行使用;他们只要确认了事实,便再不肯费心去解释其中的道理。

- **【骄矜排斥新法】**(p. 306)
  > The pride, or the policy, of the government affecting to despise any thing new or foreign, and the general want of encouragement to new inventions, however ingenious, have been greatly detrimental to the progress of the arts and manufactures. The people discover no want of genius to conceive, nor of dexterity to execute; and their imitative powers have always been acknowledged to be very great.
  ——〔译〕政府出于骄矜或权谋,做出鄙夷一切新奇之物与外来之物的姿态,又普遍不肯奖励新的发明——不论其何等精巧——这对工艺与制造的进步大为有害。至于民众,构想之才与动手之巧都不缺;其摹仿之能,更是素来公认极强。

- **【百工守旧不变】**(p. 308)
  > The want of proper encouragement from the government, and the rigid adherence to ancient usage, have rendered indeed all their fabrics stationary.
  ——〔译〕政府缺乏应有的奖励,加上死守古法,确实使他们的一切制造业都停滞不前。

- **【不用机械】**(p. 312)
  > The great advantages attainable from the use of mechanical powers are either not understood or, purposely, not employed... Whatever the reason may be, no such means are to be met with in the country.
  ——〔译〕利用机械力所能得到的巨大便益,他们或是不懂,或是有意不用。无论缘由何在,在这个国家见不到此类手段。

- **【绘画不知透视】**(p. 324)
  > With regard to painting, they can be considered in no other light than as miserable daubers... To give distance to objects on canvas, by diminishing them, by faint colouring, and by perspective, they have no sort of conception.
  ——〔译〕说到绘画,他们只能算是可怜的涂抹匠。至于在画布上用缩小形体、淡化色彩与透视之法造出远近之感,他们毫无概念。

- **【奴性摹仿】**(p. 328)
  > But in doing this, they exercise no judgment of their own. Every defect and blemish, original or accidental, they are sure to copy, being mere servile imitators, and not in the least feeling the force or the beauty of any specimen of the arts that may come before them.
  ——〔译〕然而他们这样做时,毫不运用自己的判断。凡有缺陷瑕疵,无论出自原作还是偶然造成,他们必定一并照抄;他们只是奴性的摹仿者,对于呈现在眼前的任何艺术范本,其力量与美感丝毫无所感受。

- **【全国无一雕像】**(p. 329)
  > They are as little able to model as to draw the human figure with any degree of correctness. In the whole empire there is not a statue, a hewn pillar, or a column that deserves to be mentioned.
  ——〔译〕他们塑人体与画人体一样,都不能达到些许准确。举全帝国而言,没有一尊雕像、一根雕柱或一根石柱值得一提。

- **【建筑丑陋失度】**(p. 330)
  > The whole of their architecture, indeed, is as unsightly as unsolid; without elegance or convenience of design, and without any settled proportion; mean in its appearance, and clumsy in the workmanship... of these useless and whimsical edifices His Majesty's garden at Kew exhibits a specimen, which is not inferior in any respect to the very best I have met with in China.
  ——〔译〕他们的建筑,整体上既不悦目,也不坚固;设计既无雅致,又不便用,更无一定的比例;外观寒陋,工艺笨拙。此等无用而任性的建筑物,陛下的邱园中便有一例,其在任何方面都不逊于我在中国所见的最佳之作。

- **【运河今不如昔】**(p. 336)
  > Whether it has originally been constructed by Chinese or Tartars, the conception of such an undertaking, and the manner in which it is executed, imply a degree of science and ingenuity beyond what I suspect we should now find in the country, either in one or the other of these people.
  ——〔译〕不论此工程最初出自中国人还是满人之手,如此构想与如此施工所需的学识与巧思,我怀疑今日在这个国家、在这两族之中的任何一族身上,都已找不到了。

- **【格物之学全阙】**(p. 341)
  > no branch of natural philosophy is made a study, or a pursuit in China... Of pneumatics, hydrostatics, electricity, and magnetism, they may be said to have little or no knowledge.
  ——〔译〕在中国,自然哲学没有一个分支被当作学问来研求。至于气体力学、流体静力学、电学与磁学,可以说他们所知甚少,乃至全无。

- **【不识精器之妙】**(p. 343)
  > They had little esteem for what they could not comprehend, and specimens of art served only to excite their jealousy, and to wound their pride. Whenever a future embassy shall be sent to Pekin, I should recommend articles of gold, silver, and steel, children's toys and trinkets... for in their present state, they are totally incapable of appreciating any thing great or excellent in the arts and sciences.
  ——〔译〕凡不能理解之物,他们都不加珍视;精工制品只会激起他们的妒意,伤及他们的骄傲。今后若再遣使赴北京,我主张携带金、银、钢制器物,以及儿童玩具与小饰品;因为以他们目前的状况,对技艺与科学中任何伟大精妙之物,都完全无力鉴赏。

- **【医理荒诞】**(p. 345)
  > The physiology of the human body... is neither understood, nor considered as necessary to be known; and their skill in pathology... is extremely limited, very often absurd, and generally erroneous. The seat of most diseases are, in fact, supposed to be discoverable by feeling the pulse, agreeably to a system built upon principles the most wild and extravagant.
  ——〔译〕人体生理,他们既不理解,也不认为有必要知道;其病理之术极为有限,往往荒谬,大抵错误。事实上他们以为,大多数疾病的病位都可凭切脉查明,而所依据的那套体系,建立在最荒诞离奇的原理之上。

- **【江湖术士遍地】**(p. 347)
  > The number of quacks and venders of nostrums is immense in every city who gain a livelihood by the credulity of the multitude.
  ——〔译〕每座城市里,庸医与卖秘方者多不胜数,靠着众人的轻信糊口谋生。

- **【医术不如学徒少年】**(p. 354)
  > "In the greatest, most ancient, and most civilized empire on the face of the earth, an empire that was great, populous, and highly civilized two thousand years ago, when this country was as savage as New Zealand is at present, no such good medical aid can be obtained among the people of it, as a smart boy of sixteen, who had been but twelve months apprentice to a good and well employed Edinburgh Surgeon, might reasonably be expected to afford."
  ——〔译〕"在这地球上最伟大、最古老、最文明的帝国——两千年前便已地广人众、文明高度发达,而其时我国尚如今日的新西兰一般野蛮——在这个帝国的百姓中间,却得不到这样的医疗救助:一个十六岁的机灵少年,只在一位高明而业务繁忙的爱丁堡外科医生门下当了十二个月学徒,便理当能够提供的那种救助。"

- **【琐事则大·大事则小(全书名句)】**(p. 355)
  > I think, upon the whole, it may fairly be concluded, that the Chinese have been among the first nations, now existing in the world, to arrive at a certain pitch of perfection, where, from the policy of the government, or some other cause, they have remained stationary: that they were civilized, fully to the same extent they now are, more than two thousand years ago, at a period when all Europe might be considered, comparatively, as barbarous; but that they have since made little progress in any thing, and been retrograde in many things: that, at this moment, compared with Europe, they can only be said to be great in trifles, whilst they are really trifling in every thing that is great.
  ——〔译〕总而言之,我以为可以公允地断言:中国人是当今世上最早达到某种完备程度的民族之一,而此后因政府的政策或别的缘故,他们便停滞于此;两千多年前,当全欧洲相形之下尚可视为野蛮之时,他们的文明程度已与今日无异;然而自那以后,他们在任何事上都少有进步,在许多事上更是倒退。就眼下与欧洲相较,只能说他们在琐事上伟大,而在一切伟大之事上琐屑。

## 第七章 政府与法律

- **【权柄压制民性(核心段)】**(p. 359)
  > At the present day, however, it is sufficiently evident, that the heavy hand of power has completely overcome and moulded to its own shape the physical character of the people, and that their moral sentiments and actions are swayed by the opinions, and almost under the entire dominion, of the government.
  ——〔译〕然而在今日,有一点已十分明显:权力的重手已彻底压服这个民族,并按其自身的形状将这个民族的体质品格捏塑成型;他们的道德情感与行为,皆随政府的意见而摇摆,几乎完全处在政府的支配之下。

- **【父母官实为暴虐】**(p. 360)
  > It is greatly to be lamented that a system of government, so plausible in theory, should be liable to so many abuses in practice; and that this fatherly care and affection in the governors, and filial duty and reverence in the governed would, with much more propriety, be expressed by the terms of tyranny, oppression, and injustice in the one, and by fear, deceit, and disobedience in the other.
  ——〔译〕极可叹者,一种理论上如此言之成理的政体,实行起来却百弊丛生;治人者所谓的父母般的关切与慈爱,受治者所谓的孝顺与敬畏,若改用另一套词来称呼,反倒更为贴切:前者是暴虐、压迫与不义,后者是恐惧、欺诈与不服从。

- **【人主深居不出】**(p. 360)
  > The first grand maxim on which the Emperor acts is, seldom to appear before the public, a maxim whose origin would be difficultly traced to any principle of affection or solicitude for his children; much more easily explained as the offspring of suspicion. The tyrant who may be conscious of having committed, or assented to, acts of cruelty and oppression, must feel a reluctance to mix with those who may have smarted under the lash of his power.
  ——〔译〕皇帝行事的头一条大原则,是绝少在公众面前露面。此一原则的由来,很难归之于对其"子民"的慈爱或挂念,而远更容易解释为猜忌的产物。一个自知曾犯下或曾默许残暴与压迫之举的暴君,自然不愿与那些可能在其鞭下呻吟过的人混在一处。

- **【罪刑轻重失当】**(p. 368)
  > In attempting to proportion punishments to the degrees of crimes, indeed of awarding the same punishment for stealing a loaf of bread and taking away the life of man, the Chinese legislators, according to our notions, seem to have made too little distinction between accidental manslaughter and premeditated murder... If a man should kill another by an unforeseen and unavoidable accident, his life is forfeited by the law.
  ——〔译〕在使刑罚与罪行轻重相称这件事上——偷一块面包与夺人性命竟判以同样的刑罚——依我们的观念看,中国的立法者对过失致死与预谋杀人几乎不作区分。倘一人因不可预见、不可避免的意外而致他人死亡,依法亦须以命相抵。

- **【株连之酷】**(p. 373)
  > so in China, even to the ninth generation, a traitor's blood is supposed to be tainted... Nothing can be more unjust and absurd, however politic, than such a law, absurd, because it considers a non-entity capable of committing a crime; and unjust, because it punishes an innocent person.
  ——〔译〕在中国,叛逆者的血脉被视为直到第九代仍属污秽。此种法律,无论在权术上如何有用,再没有比它更不义、更荒谬的了:荒谬,因为它认定一个并不存在的人也能犯罪;不义,因为它惩罚无辜之人。

- **【官吏受贿枉法】**(p. 377)
  > where the officers of justice are known, in most cases, to be corrupted by bribery.
  ——〔译〕在那里,司法官吏众所公认在多数情形下都已被贿赂所腐蚀。

- **【民讼无可上诉】**(p. 377)
  > The sentence in such causes being thus left in the breast of a single judge... the exclusion from appeal is in itself a bar to the just and impartial administration of the laws. The subject being refused the benefit of carrying his cause into a higher... court, has no security against the caprice, malice, or corruption of his judge.
  ——〔译〕此类案件的判决既全凭一名法官的胸臆而定,而上诉之路又被堵绝,这本身便阻断了法律的公正无偏之施行。百姓既不得将案子申诉到更高一级的法庭,对于其审判官的任性、恶意或贪贿,便毫无保障可言。

- **【刑讯逼供】**(p. 379)
  > The abominable practice of extorting confession by the application of the torture is the worst part of the criminal laws of China... It is however a common punishment to squeeze the fingers in cases of misdemeanour.
  ——〔译〕以刑求逼取口供这一可憎的做法,是中国刑法中最恶劣的部分。至于轻罪案件,夹挤手指乃是常用之刑。

- **【人治高于法治】**(p. 380)
  > The avarice of men in power may overlook those who are in moderate circumstances, but the affluent rarely escape their rapacious grasp... The executive administration is so faulty, that the man in office generally has it in his power to govern the laws, which makes the measure of good or evil depend greatly on his moral character.
  ——〔译〕当权者的贪欲或许会放过家境平常之人,但富有者极少能逃脱其贪婪的掌握。行政之败坏已至于此:在位之人一般都有本事支配法律,于是善恶的尺度便大半取决于他个人的品性。

- **【笞责任意无审】**(p. 380)
  > The officers of the several departments of government, from the first to the ninth degree, acting upon the same broad basis of paternal authority, are invested with the power of inflicting the summary punishment of the bamboo, on all occasions where they may judge it proper, which, under the denomination of a fatherly correction, they administer without any previous trial, or form of inquiry. The slightest offence is punishable in this manner, at the will or the caprice of the lowest magistrate.
  ——〔译〕各衙门自一品至九品的官员,同样依据父权这一宽泛的根据,皆被授予随时施加竹板笞责之权,只要他们认为合宜即可动手;此举名曰"父亲式的教训",施行时既不经审理,亦无任何查问的程式。最轻微的过失也可照此惩治,全凭最低级官吏的意愿或一时兴致。

- **【受杖谢恩·满汉之别(全文)】**(p. 381–382)
  > In travelling through the country, a day seldom escaped without our witnessing the application of the Pan-tsé or bamboo, and generally in such a manner that it might be called by any other name except a gentle correction. A Chinese suffering under this punishment cries out in the most piteous manner; a Tartar bears it in silence. A Chinese, after receiving a certain number of strokes, falls down on his knees, as a matter [382] of course, before him who ordered the punishment, thanking him, in the most humble manner, for the fatherly kindness he has testified towards his son, in thus putting him in mind of his errors; a Tartar grumbles, and disputes the point as to the right that a Chinese may have to flog him; or he turns away in sullen silence.
  ——〔译〕我们行经此国,几乎没有一天不亲见板子(Pan-tsé)即竹杖的施用,而且其施用之状,叫它什么都可以,唯独不能叫作"温和的教训"。受此刑的汉人哭喊得极为可怜;韃靼人则默然承受。汉人在受了一定数目的杖击之后,理所当然地跪倒在下令责打者面前,以最卑屈的方式叩谢他对"儿子"所示的父亲般的恩德——竟以此法提醒他改正过错;韃靼人则嘟囔抱怨,争辩一个汉人究竟有何权利打他,或者阴沉着脸一声不响地转身走开。

- **【举国奴颜婢膝(核心段·全文)】**(p. 382–383)
  > Ridiculous as it may appear to a foreigner, in observing an officer of state stretching himself along the ground for the purpose of being flogged by order of another who happens to rank one degree above him; yet it is impossible, at the same time, to suppress a glow of indignation, in witnessing so mean and obsequious a degradation of the human mind, which can bring itself, under any circumstances, patiently to submit to a vile corporal punishment, administered by the hand of a slave, or by a common soldier; and when this is done, to undergo the still more vile and humiliating act of kissing the rod that corrects him. But the policy of the government has taken good care to remove any scruples that might arise on this score. Where paternal regard was the sole motive, such a chastisement could not possibly be followed with dishonour or disgrace. It was a wonderful point gained by the government, to subject every individual, the Emperor only excepted, to the same corporal correction; but it must have required great address, and men's minds must have been completely subdued, or completely convinced, before such a system of universal obedience could have been accomplished, the consequence of which, it was obvious, could be no other than universal servility. It could not fail to establish a most effectual check against the complaints of the multitude, by shewing them that [383] the same man, who had the power of punishing them, was equally liable to be corrected in his turn, and in the same manner, by another.
  ——〔译〕一名朝廷官员俯身伏地,只为受另一位恰好高他一级者下令的鞭责:此情此景在外国人看来固然可笑;然而同时又不能不油然生出一股愤慨,因为所见者乃是人心如此卑贱、如此谄媚的堕落——竟能在任何情形之下都甘心忍受一名奴仆或一名寻常兵丁之手所施的下贱体罚;而且受罚之后,还要行一桩更为下贱、更为屈辱之事:亲吻那责打自己的杖。然而政府的策术早已周到地替人们除去了在这一点上可能产生的顾虑:既然唯一的动机是父亲式的关爱,这样的责罚便决不可能带来羞辱或耻辱。政府赢得的一大妙着,便是使除皇帝一人之外的每一个人都同受此种体罚;但要成就这样一套举国服从的制度,必曾费过极大的手段,人心也必已被彻底压服,或被彻底说服——而其后果显然只能是举国的奴颜婢膝。它必然成为压制众人怨言的最有效的约束,因为它向众人表明:那个有权责打他们的人,同样随时可能轮到自己被另一个人以同样的方式教训。

- **【笞刑之古·先于文明】**(p. 383)
  > The punishment of the bamboo must, I suspect, be one of the most ancient institutions of China. Indeed we can scarcely conceive it ever to have been introduced into a society already civilized; but rather to have been coeval with the origin of that society.
  ——〔译〕我猜想,竹板之刑必是中国最古老的制度之一。我们实在难以设想它会被引入一个已经开化的社会;毋宁说,它与那个社会的起源同时。

- **【俄国笞责之比较】**(p. 383)
  > A similar kind of personal chastisement for light offences, or misconduct, was inflicted in Russia on persons of all ranks, but with this difference, that the correction was private and by order of the Sovereign alone. The Czar Peter, indeed, generally bestowed a drubbing on his courtiers with his own hand; who, instead of being dishonoured or disgraced by such a castigation, were supposed, from that very circumstance, to be his...
  ——〔译〕在俄国,对轻罪或失当行为也曾对各等级之人施以类似的体罚,但有一点不同:这种教训是私下进行的,且只能由君主一人下令。彼得沙皇确实常常亲手痛打他的朝臣;而这些人并不因此种责打而蒙羞失体,恰恰相反,正因如此才被认为是他的……

- **【中俄之比·老病与少壮】**(p. 384)
  > One century ago Russia was but just emerging from a state of barbarism, and in a century hence, in all human probability, she will make a conspicuous figure among European nations, both in arts and arms. Two thousand years ago China was civilized to the same degree, or nearly so, that she is at present. The governments were both arbitrary, and the people were slaves. The one is in a state of youthful vigour, advancing daily in strength and knowledge; the other is worn out with old age and disease, and under its present state of existence is not likely to advance in any kind of improvement.
  ——〔译〕一百年前,俄国方才脱出野蛮之境;而再过一百年,以人事之常理度之,她在文艺与武备两方面都将于欧洲列邦中占据显赫的地位。两千年前,中国的文明程度已与今日相同,或相去不远。两国的政府同属专断,两国的百姓同为奴隶。然而一国正当少壮之年,力量与知识日有增进;另一国则已被衰老与疾病磨蚀殆尽,在其现今的生存状态之下,恐无望在任何方面有所进步。

- **【和珅之贪】**(p. 387)
  > he availed himself of the means that offered, by every species of fraud and extortion, by tyranny and oppression, to amass such immense wealth in gold, silver, pearls, and immoveable property, that his acquisitions were generally allowed to have exceeded those of any single individual, that the history of the country had made known.
  ——〔译〕他(和珅,Ho-chang-tong)利用一切可乘之机,以种种欺诈勒索、以暴虐压迫之手段,聚敛金银、珠宝与不动产,财富之巨,公认超过该国有史以来任何一人之所得。

- **【官为虐民之首·无中间阶层】**(p. 389)
  > The officers of government in general, though intended by the constitution as a kind of barrier between the prince and the people, are the greatest oppressors of the latter, who have seldom any means of redress... There is no middle class of men in China... In fact, there are no other than the governors and the governed. If a man, by trade, or industry in his profession, has accumulated riches, he can enjoy them only in private. He dares not, by having a grander house, or finer clothes, to let his neighbour perceive that he is richer than himself.
  ——〔译〕政府官吏虽按国家体制本应充当君主与百姓之间的一道屏障,却恰是百姓最大的压迫者,而百姓极少有申冤之途。中国没有中间阶层……事实上,除治人者与受治者之外,别无他类。一个人若因经商或勤于本业而积得财富,他也只能在私下享用。他不敢盖更气派的房子、穿更讲究的衣服,让邻人看出他比自己富有。

- **【宪书邸报为御民之器】**(p. 391)
  > The ingenious Mr. Pauw has observed, that China is entirely governed by the whip and the bamboo. To these he might have added the yearly calendar and the Pekin Gazette, both of which, as engines in the hands of government, contribute very materially to assist its operations... Yet it is famous for describing battles that were never fought, and for announcing victories that were never gained.
  ——〔译〕机敏的帕乌先生(Mr. Pauw)曾指出,中国完全是靠鞭子与竹板来统治的。他本还可以加上时宪书与《京报》两样:二者作为政府手中的器械,对其运作的助益极为实在。……然而《京报》尤以叙述从未打过的仗、宣布从未获得的胜利而著名。

- **【无须禁书·杖责足矣】**(p. 393)
  > In China the liberty of the press seems to excite no apprehensions in the government. The summary mode of punishing any breach of good morals, without the formality of a trial, makes a positive prohibition against printing unnecessary, being itself sufficient to restrain the licentiousness of the press. The printer, the vender, and the reader of any libellous publication, are all equally liable to be flogged with the bamboo.
  ——〔译〕在中国,出版自由似乎并不引起政府的忧虑。凡有败坏风化之举,一概不经审理程式即行惩治,这就使明令禁止印书成为不必要,因为这本身已足以约束出版的放肆。凡诽谤性刊物的印者、卖者与读者,同样都可被竹板笞责。

- **【民不知有其权】**(p. 396)
  > Contented in having no voice in the government, it has never occurred to them that they have any rights: and they certainly enjoy none but what are liable to be invaded and trampled on, whenever the sovereign, or any of his representatives, from interest, malice, or caprice, think fit to exercise the power that is within their grasp. The doctrine of employing resistence against oppression... is so contrary to every sentiment of the former, that the latter has little to fear on that score.
  ——〔译〕他们安于在国政中毫无发言之权,从未想到自己拥有任何权利;而他们所享有的,也确实无一不是随时可被侵犯、被践踏的——只要君主或其任何一位代表出于利益、恶意或一时兴致,认为不妨动用握在手中的权力。以反抗对付压迫的道理,与前者(百姓)的全部情感如此相违,以致后者(统治者)在这一点上几无可惧。

- **【军容荏弱】**(p. 409)
  > In some places, where they were drawn out in compliment to the Embassador, when the weather happened to be a little warm, they were employed in the exercise of their fans, instead of their matchlocks; others we found drawn up in a single line, and resting very composedly on their knees to receive the Embassador... Their quilted petticoats, sattin boots, and their fans, had a mixture of clumsiness and effeminacy that ill accorded with the military character.
  ——〔译〕在有些地方,兵士为向公使致敬而列队,遇天气略暖,他们操演的便是扇子而非火绳枪;另有些地方,我们见他们排成单行,极其从容地跪着迎接公使。……他们的棉裙、缎靴与扇子,兼有笨拙与脂粉气,与军人的品格殊不相称。

## 第八章 宗教与源流

- **【骄鄙无知·官皆暴君民皆奴】**(p. 419)
  > and to take leave of a people, whose general character seemed to be strongly marked with pride, meanness, and ignorance. After having passed some time in a nation, where every petty officer is a tyrant, and every man a slave, how doubly precious do the blessings of that true liberty appear, which our happy constitution affords to every one the means of enjoying at home; where property is secured from violence, and where the life of the meanest subject is equally protected with that of the prince.
  ——〔译〕终于要辞别这样一个民族了,其普遍性格分明刻着骄傲、卑劣与无知三重印记。在一个每个小吏都是暴君、每个人都是奴隶的国度里住过一段时日之后,我们幸运的宪制在本国赋予每个人享受的那种真正自由,其恩泽显得加倍可贵:在那里财产不受暴力侵夺,最卑微的臣民的性命与君王的性命受到同等保护。

- **【庙宇藏污纳垢】**(p. 422)
  > Sometimes, also, the whole building is made a common place of resort for vagrants and idlers, where gamblers mix with gods, and priests with pick-pockets.
  ——〔译〕有时整座庙宇沦为游民闲汉的聚集之所:赌徒与神像杂处,僧人与扒手为伍。

- **【举国愚昧】**(p. 449)
  > the mass of the people, from the nature of the language, the maxims of the government, and other circumstances, have always been kept in a state of profound ignorance.
  ——〔译〕由于文字本身的性质、政府奉行的信条以及其他种种情形,民众始终被维持在极度无知的状态之中。

- **【孔子好占卜】**(p. 454)
  > his metaphysics are so obscure as not to be intelligible... In his writings appears a strong predilection for a kind of fortune-telling, or predicting events by the mystical lines of Fo-shee... he was cautious enough to wrap them up in such ambiguous and mysterious expressions that, like most prophecies of the kind, they might admit of a variety of interpretations.
  ——〔译〕孔子的玄学晦涩到不可理解的程度。其著述中显露出对一种占卜之术的强烈偏好,即凭伏羲的神秘卦画来预言祸福。他很谨慎地把这些话裹在含混玄虚的措辞里,于是像多数此类预言一样,可以被任意解释。

- **【以迷信固君权】**(p. 455)
  > Being prime minister of one of the kings of China, it was necessary for him to act the politician as well as the philosopher; and he could not fail to know, that the superstitions of the people were among the best supports of the government.
  ——〔译〕身为中国某位君主的宰相,他不得不既做哲人又做政客;他不会不明白,民众的迷信正是政权最有力的支柱之一。

- **【僧道欺愚牟利】**(p. 460)
  > But the priests, who, in all ages and in most nations, have been crafty enough to turn to their own account the credulity and superstitions of the people...
  ——〔译〕然而僧侣之辈,在各个时代、多数民族中,都精明到足以把民众的轻信与迷信变作自家的进项……

- **【道术无稽】**(p. 466)
  > they practice also a number of incantations, magic, invocations of spirits, and other mystical rites that are probably as little understood by themselves as by the gazing multitude.
  ——〔译〕他们还施行种种咒语、法术、召神以及别的神秘仪节,而这些名堂他们自己所懂的,恐怕与围观的众人一样少。

- **【偶像近于野蛮】**(p. 472)
  > whenever any unusual calamity befals a town or village... by way of punishing the gods, they literally pull down the temple over their heads, and leave them sitting in the open air. The grotesque and barbarous manner of representing the manifold powers of nature... is by no means favourable to the supposition of a refined or superior understanding in the people who adopt them into their religious worship. It can be considered only as a very short step beyond the conceptions of savages.
  ——〔译〕每逢城镇乡村遭遇非常之灾,他们就惩罚神明:把庙宇拆掉,让神像露天而坐。至于以那样怪诞野蛮的形象来表现自然的种种力量,绝不足以让人推想采用这类偶像作崇拜对象的民族有何精微或高明的智识。这只能算是比野蛮人的观念稍稍向前迈了一小步而已。

- **【僧侣不学无术】**(p. 476)
  > No information as to the ground-work of their religion is to be looked for from the priests of the present day, who are generally very ignorant.
  ——〔译〕关于其宗教的根本义理,不必指望从今日的僧侣口中得到什么,因为他们大抵极其无知。

- **【妇女求神算命】**(p. 480)
  > Barren wives are even encouraged to visit the temples, not so much for the purpose of knowing their destiny, as under a firm belief that, by rubbing the bellies of certain little copper gods, they shall conceive and bear children... A Chinese, even in the higher ranks, has no great idea of a man's learning, if he be ignorant of the Swan-ming.
  ——〔译〕不育的妇人甚至被鼓励前往庙中,倒不是为了探问命运,而是笃信只要摩擦某些小铜神像的肚子,便能怀胎生子。一个中国人,即便身居上层,若某人不懂算命,便不认为此人有什么学问。

- **【中国人不祈祷】**(p. 486)
  > the primitive religion of China no longer exists, or exists only in a corrupted state; that there is at present no national nor scarcely a state religion... in all of which the people appear to be rather actuated by the dread of evil in this life, than by the fear of punishment in another... for a Chinese can scarcely be said to pray; he is grateful when the event proves favourable to his wishes; petulant and peevish with his gods when adverse.
  ——〔译〕中国原始的宗教已不复存在,或仅以败坏的形态残留;今日既无民族宗教,也几乎谈不上国家宗教。在这一切当中,民众似乎是被今生的祸患之惧所驱动,而非畏惧来世的惩罚。中国人几乎不能说是在祈祷:事情如愿,他便感恩;事情不遂,他就对自己的神明发脾气、生闷气。

## 第九章 南下广州途中:民生、农业、饥荒

- **【茅屋破败】**(p. 494)
  > we were rather surprized at the fewness of these, as well as at the very ruinous and miserable condition of almost all the cottages. These mean huts were built, some of half-burnt bricks and others of clay, and they were thatched with the straw of grain or with reeds.
  ——〔译〕我们颇为惊讶:这样的农舍数目既少,而且几乎无一不破败不堪、景况凄惨。这些寒陋的窝棚,有的用半生不熟的砖砌成,有的用泥土垒起,屋顶则以谷秸或芦苇覆盖。

- **【争食残羹茶渣】**(p. 495)
  > The appearance, indeed, of all the peasantry in this province was marked with every indication of poverty... With the greatest thankfulness they received the offals of our allowance; and the tea-leaves, which we had used, were sought after by them with avidity and boiled up for their beverage.
  ——〔译〕这一省农民的模样,处处都是贫穷的印记。他们对我们吃剩的下脚残食感激不尽;连我们泡过的茶叶,他们也争相索取,拿回去煮水当饮品。

- **【纤夫老弱如病院】**(p. 502)
  > Most of them consisted of infirm and decrepit old men, and the rest were such lank, sickly-looking, ill-clothed creatures, that the whole groupe appeared to be much fitter for an hospital than for performing any kind of labour.
  ——〔译〕他们大半是体弱龙钟的老人,其余的也都干瘦病容、衣不蔽体,整整一群人,看上去更适合进医院,而不是去干任何一种活计。

- **【专制驱使百万】**(p. 512)
  > The quantity of human labour that must have been employed... could not have been supplied, in any reasonable length of time, except in a country where millions could be set to work at the nod of a despot. The greatest works in China have always been, and still continue to be, performed by the accumulation of manual labour, without the assistance of machinery.
  ——〔译〕此项工程所必耗的人力,若非在一个专制君主颔首之间便能驱动百万人的国度,绝无可能在任何合乎情理的期限内凑齐。中国最浩大的工程,向来是、且至今仍是靠人力的层层堆积完成的,毫无机械之助。

- **【道路不如文明诸国】**(p. 513)
  > In this respect they fall short of most civilized nations. Except near the capital... there is scarcely a road in the whole country that can be ranked beyond a foot-path.
  ——〔译〕在这一点上,他们比大多数文明国家都差得远。除京城近郊之外,全国几乎没有一条道路称得上比小径更好。

- **【终岁劳而仅足糊口】**(p. 531)
  > With all this industry it might be concluded, from the general appearance of the people, that they merely gained a subsistence.
  ——〔译〕尽管如此勤苦,从百姓的普遍形貌看来,只能断定他们所得仅够勉强糊口而已。

- **【鄱阳湖之荒凉】**(p. 533)
  > This place may justly be considered as the sink of China... It is scarcely possible for the imagination to form to itself an idea of a more desolate region than that which surrounds the Po-yang lake.
  ——〔译〕此地可以名正言顺地视为中国的污水坑。想象力也几乎无从构想出比鄱阳湖四周更荒凉的地带。

- **【京畿之民最苦】**(p. 545)
  > It was a remark too singular to escape notice that, except in the neighbourhood of the Po-yang lake, the peasantry of the province in which the capital stands were more miserable, their houses more mean and wretched, and their lands in a worse state of cultivation, than in any other part of the route.
  ——〔译〕有一点太过奇特,不容不加注意:除鄱阳湖一带外,京城所在之省的农民,比我们沿途所经任何地方都更为困苦,房舍更为寒陋破败,田地耕作状况也更差。

- **【食粗形槁】**(p. 546)
  > The poverty of their food was sufficiently indicated by their meagre appearance. It consists chiefly of boiled rice, millet, or other grain, with the addition of onions or garlic, and mixed sometimes with a few other vegetables that, by way of relish, are fried in rancid oil.
  ——〔译〕他们形容枯槁,足以说明饮食之贫薄。所食主要是煮熟的稻米、小米或别种谷物,加些葱蒜,有时再掺几样蔬菜,为求下饭,用变味的油炒过。

- **【无乳无酪无面包】**(p. 547)
  > A morsel of pork to relish their rice is almost the only kind of meat that the poor can afford to taste. They have little milk and neither butter, nor cheese, nor bread; articles of nourishment to which, with potatoes, the peasantry of Europe owe their chief support.
  ——〔译〕一小块猪肉用以下饭,几乎是穷人唯一吃得上的荤腥。他们少有牛乳,既无黄油,也无干酪,更无面包——而欧洲农民的主要口粮,正是靠这几样再加上马铃薯。

- **【冻馁而死·鬻儿求活】**(p. 551)
  > The summers are so warm that they go nearly naked and the winters so severe that, what with their poor and scanty fare, their want of fuel, clothing, and even shelter, thousands are said to perish from cold and hunger. In such a condition the ties of nature sometimes yield to self-preservation, and children are sold to save both the parent and offspring from perishing for want; and infants become a prey to hopeless indigence.
  ——〔译〕夏天酷热,他们几乎赤身裸体;冬天严寒,加以食物粗劣匮乏,又缺燃料、衣裳,乃至栖身之所,据说每年冻饿而死者以千计。在这般境地里,天性之情有时让位于自保:儿女被卖掉,以使父母与子女都免于饿死;而婴孩则成了无望穷困的牺牲品。

- **【百姓形如稻草人】**(p. 553)
  > It would be rare to find, among the commonalty of China, one to compare with a porter-drinking citizen or a jolly-looking farmer of England. They are indeed naturally of a slender habit of body and a sickly appearance, few having the blush of health upon their cheeks.
  ——〔译〕在中国的平民百姓中,很难找出一个能与英格兰那种喝黑啤的市民、或红光满面的快活农夫相比的人。他们本就体格瘦弱、面带病容,颊上鲜见健康的血色。

- **【农业不及欧洲】**(p. 567)
  > As horticulturists they may perhaps be allowed a considerable share of merit; but, on the great scale of agriculture, they are certainly not to be mentioned with many European nations. They have no knowledge of the modes of improvement practised in the various breeds of cattle; no instruments for breaking up and preparing waste lands; no system for draining and reclaiming swamps and morasses... For want of this knowledge, a very considerable portion of the richest land, perhaps, in the whole empire, is suffered to remain a barren and unprofitable waste.
  ——〔译〕作为园艺者,或可承认他们颇有可取之处;但论大规模农业,他们断不足与许多欧洲国家相提并论。他们不懂各类牲畜品种的改良之法,没有开垦生地、整治荒土的器具,也没有排水和围垦沼泽泥泞之地的办法。正因缺此知识,全帝国或许最肥沃的土地中,有相当大一部分被听任荒废,毫无产出。

- **【饥荒·卖子弑亲】**(p. 585)
  > Insurrection and rebellion ensue, and those who may escape the devouring scourge of famine, in all probability, fall by the sword. In such seasons a whole province is sometimes half depopulated; wretched parents are reduced, by imperious want, to sell or destroy their offspring, and children to put an end, by violence, to the sufferings of their aged and infirm parents.
  ——〔译〕于是民变与叛乱继起,凡侥幸逃脱饥荒吞噬之灾者,多半又死于刀兵。逢此年岁,一整省人口有时减去一半;可怜的父母为势所迫,不得不卖掉或杀掉自己的儿女,而儿女则以暴力手段,了结年老病弱双亲的苦难。

## 第十章 广东与结论

- **【南方民风丕变·呼为番鬼】**(p. 592)
  > even the peasantry ran out of their houses, as we passed, and bawled after us Queitze-fan-quei, which, in their language, are opprobrious and contemptuous expressions, signifying foreign devils, imps; epithets that are bestowed by the enlightened Chinese on all foreigners... In Quan-tung the farther we advanced, the more rude and insolent they became.
  ——〔译〕我们经过时，连乡间农人也奔出屋外，在背后高声叫喊"鬼子""番鬼"。此二语在他们口中乃辱骂轻蔑之词，意为外国的魔鬼、妖物；开明的中国人便以这等称号加诸一切外国人。在广东境内，我们越往前行，他们就越发粗野无礼。

- **【下民亦轻外人】**(p. 592)
  > This contempt of foreigners is not confined to the upper ranks, or men in office, but pervades the very lowest class who... affect, at the same time, to despise their employers, and to consider them as placed, in the scale of human beings, many degrees below them.
  ——〔译〕这种轻视外国人的态度并不限于上层或有官职之人，而是渗透到最下等的阶层。这些人一面为外国人所雇，一面却摆出鄙夷雇主的样子，认为在人类的品级上，雇主比自己低下许多等。

- **【仆役掺茶欺洋】**(p. 593)
  > "And is that the way," said I, "in which you cheat your own countrymen?" "No," replied he, "my own countrymen are too wise to be so easily cheated, but your's are stupid enough to let serve you such like tricks; and indeed," continued he, with the greatest sang froid imaginable, "anything you get from us is quite good enough for you."
  ——〔译〕我说："你们就是这样欺骗自己的同胞么？"他答道："不，我的同胞太精明，不会这样轻易受骗；倒是你们的人蠢，尽可以用这类手法对付。"他又以想象得到的最从容的神气接着说："说实在的，凡是从我们这儿拿到的东西，给你们已经很够好了。"

- **【官纵娼而分其利】**(p. 595)
  > for which, however, they had not only the consent and approbation of their parents, but also the sanction of the government, or perhaps, to speak more correctly, of the governing magistrates, given in consideration of their receiving a portion of the wages of prostitution.
  ——〔译〕不过她们干此事不但得到父母的同意与赞许，还得到政府的准许——或者更确切地说，是得到管事官员的准许；而官员之所以准许，是因为他们可以从卖淫所得中分取一份。

- **【赈银层层剥蚀】**(p. 607)
  > The Emperor having once lodged at the place immediately ordered 100,000 ounces of silver for their relief, out of which the first officer of the treasury took 20,000, the second 10,000, the third 5,000, and so on, till at last there remained only 20,000 for the poor sufferers. So that the boasted morality of China is pretty much the same, when reduced to practice, as that of other countries.
  ——〔译〕皇帝曾在该地驻跸，遂即刻下旨拨银十万两赈济。然而库中头一位官员先取去二万，第二位取一万，第三位取五千，如此层层剥蚀，最后留给受灾贫民的只剩二万两。可见中国人自诩的道德，一旦见诸实行，与别国并无多少分别。

- **【何必来我国】**(p. 610)
  > The common answer is, "Why do you come here? We take in exchange your articles of produce and manufacture, which we really have no occasion for, and give you in return our precious tea, which nature has denied to your country, and yet you are not satisfied. Why do you so often visit a country whose customs you dislike?... Respect then our hospitality, but don't pretend to regulate or reform it."
  ——〔译〕他们惯常的回答是："你们何必来此？我们收下你们的土产与制品——那些东西我们其实并不需要——却把贵重的茶叶给你们，那是造化不曾赐予你们国土的；你们竟仍不知足。既然厌恶一国的风俗，为何还屡屡前来？……那就敬重我们的款待罢，不要妄想加以管束或改革。"

- **【行商垄断勒索】**(p. 610)
  > This is still more likely to happen from the complete monopoly of all foreign trade being consigned to a limited number of merchants, seldom, I believe, exceeding eight, who are sanctioned by government.
  ——〔译〕此种情形之所以更易发生，是因为全部对外贸易的垄断权被交付给为数有限的商人——据我所知，其数很少超过八家——而这些人是经政府核准的。

- **【三年清知府】**(p. 611)
  > The principal officers of this government are invariably sent down from Pekin; they arrive poor and, in the course of three years, return with immense riches.
  ——〔译〕此地政府的主要官员一概由北京派来；他们到任时两手空空，三年之间便携巨富而归。

- **【真珠易以赝品】**(p. 612)
  > his mortification may easily be supposed, on discovering that his real pearl had been exchanged for an artificial one, so very like as not to be detected but by the most critical examination.
  ——〔译〕当他发觉自己那颗真珠已被换成一颗假珠，而假珠又极为逼真，非经最苛细的查验不能辨识，其懊恼之情可以想见。

- **【外人难得公道】**(p. 613)
  > It has always been considered that a foreigner has little chance of obtaining justice at Canton. The import and export duties, which by the law of the country ought to be levied ad valorem, are arbitrarily fixed according to the fancy of the collector... to be honest, when power and opportunity lend their aid to roguery, is a virtue not within the pale of Chinese morality.
  ——〔译〕人们向来认为，外国人在广州极少有获得公道的机会。进出口税本应依该国法律按值抽征，实际却全凭征收官的心意任意定额……当权力与机会都为奸欺之行助势时，仍能守正不欺，这样的德行并不在中国道德的范围之内。

- **【关税不入国库】**(p. 613)
  > we may conclude how very small a proportion of the duties, extorted from foreigners trading to Canton, finds its way into the Imperial treasury.
  ——〔译〕由此可以推断，从赴广州贸易的外国人身上勒索来的税款，最终流入皇家国库的只占极小的一部分。

- **【吏治积弊】**(p. 614)
  > Thus the taxes, which... are extremely moderate, by the abuses of the administration become serious grievances to the foreign merchant.
  ——〔译〕于是，税额本身原极轻微，却因吏治的种种积弊，成为外国商人严重的苦累。

- **【杀人立决不经审】**(p. 620)
  > Hitherto the Chinese have invariably made a point of executing immediately, and without a regular trial, any foreigner who should kill a Chinese, or some substitute in the place of the actual criminal.
  ——〔译〕迄今为止，凡有外国人杀死中国人，中国人一概坚持立即处死该外国人，并不经正式审判；或者索性以他人抵命，代替真正的凶犯。

- **【以欧洲尺度衡之】**(p. 621)
  > The comparisons I have made were given with a view of assisting the reader to form in his own mind some idea what rank the Chinese may be considered to hold, when measured by the scale of European nations.
  ——〔译〕我所作的这些比较，意在帮助读者自行判断：若以欧洲各国为尺度来衡量，中国人可算处于何等地位。

## 一、马戛尔尼(Lord Macartney),使团日记,1793–94

**破船之喻**

> The Empire of China is an old, crazy, first rate Man of War, which a fortunate succession of able and vigilant officers have contrived to keep afloat for these hundred and fifty years past, and to overawe their neighbours merely by her bulk and appearance. But whenever an insufficient man happens to have the command upon deck, adieu to the discipline and safety of the ship.

——〔译〕中华帝国是一艘破旧而疯癫的头等战舰,幸赖一连串能干而警觉的船长,勉强让它在过去一百五十年间浮在水面上,仅凭庞大的身躯与外表就震慑住了四邻。可是一旦甲板上掌舵的碰巧是个不称职的人,这艘船的纪律与安全就都要告别了。

(通行版本续有:她或许不会立即沉没,可能作为残骸漂流一时,终将在岸上撞得粉碎,但绝无可能在旧船底上重建。见 J. L. Cranmer-Byng 编 *An Embassy to China: Lord Macartney's Journal*, 1962。)

**"半野蛮"一条**:马戛尔尼另有一段大意为——自满洲入关以来的一百五十年间,中国没有前进反而后退;当我们日日在技艺与科学上前进时,他们相形之下已变成半野蛮人。此条英文原文我未能核实到可靠出处,暂列待考,请勿作定本引用。

**语境**:马戛尔尼是拒绝三跪九叩的那个人,日记写于使团失败之后。破船之喻是英国需要的一套关于中国的解释——说明这个帝国不值得敬畏,也不必平等相待。四十年后它成了炮舰的注脚。但马戛尔尼本人的评价远比巴罗持平,日记中亦有诸多赞赏之语;斯当东爵士的官方报告更为客气,巴罗写书的动机之一正是纠正这份客气。

## 二、林德利(Augustus F. Lindley,笔名 Lin-Le),《太平天国》(*Ti-Ping Tien-Kwoh*),伦敦 1866,两卷

**满清统治摧毁一切**(卷一)

> the grinding oppression of nearly two centuries had apparently obliterated all that was good and noble in the land, and the debasing influence of the Manchoo invaders seemed likely to consummate the entire destruction of the moral, social, and political condition of the Chinese.

——〔译〕近两百年来那种碾磨般的压迫,似已把这片土地上一切良善高贵的东西抹得干净;而满洲入侵者那种使人堕落的影响,看来将要把中国人在道德、社会与政治上的境况彻底摧毁殆尽。

**人如伏地之豕**(卷二)

> From the day these Tartars came into the country, China has been steadily deteriorating, and now the people may best be likened to herds of grovelling swine, living merely for the day, stultified in intellect by the most degrading superstition.

——〔译〕自这些鞑靼人入境的那一天起,中国就一直在稳步退化;如今这个民族最恰当的比方,是一群群伏地而行的猪,只为眼前一日而活,心智被最下等的迷信弄得愚钝不堪。

**世代习于血与刑**(卷二)

> From infancy the people have become habituated to scenes of blood and torture, similar to those inflicted upon their ancestors during the last two centuries by the Tartar conquerors. Made callous and degraded by the ceaseless persecution of their authorities; unnaturally branded with the shaven-headed badge of slavery; their spirit broken and debased by a system of grinding tyranny; their lives and property at the mercy of the most merciless officials in existence … seclusion is their salvation; too surely they know that their power consists in the weakness, ignorance, superstition, and degradation of their Chinese slaves. … One of the most remarkable contrasts between the Ti-pings and their enslaved countrymen … One presents a type of the whole—a dull, apathetic countenance, without expression or intelligence, except what resembles the half-cunning, half-fearful manner of slaves … Where you would see the servile Tartar-subdued Chinamen continually cringing …

——〔译〕这个民族自襁褓之中便已习惯于流血与酷刑的场面——与两百年来鞑靼征服者加在他们祖先身上的那些一样。官府无休无止的迫害使他们麻木而卑下;剃发这一奴隶的标记违逆天性地烙在他们身上;碾磨般的暴政这一整套制度,把他们的精神打断、压低;他们的性命与财产,悉听当世最无情的官吏摆布……闭关自守就是他们的救命之道;他们太清楚了,自己的权力正建立在中国奴隶的孱弱、无知、迷信与卑下之上。……太平军与他们那些做了奴隶的同胞之间,最触目的对照之一……后者是这全体的一个典型:一张迟钝冷漠的面孔,毫无表情、毫无智慧,所有的只是一种半狡黠半畏惧的奴才气……你看到的是那些被鞑靼驯服的、奴颜婢膝的中国人,永远在缩着身子。

**屠戮甲于古今**(卷一)

> The enormous multitude of victims slaughtered during the progress and maintenance of the Manchoo dynasty will never be known by Europeans; though—judging by all authentic records of their invasion of China, its constant rebellions against their authority, and the murderous rule they have exercised—the destruction of life considerably outnumbered the hosts sacrificed in the track of the greatest destroyers of the human species upon record, from Alexander the Great to Genghis-Khan. The barbarity of the Manchoo rule is unparalleled in ancient or modern history; while the fiendish nature of their punishments by torture—especially those for treason—and the records of the "board of punishments," instituted by them, constitute the blackest spot in the annals of mankind.

——〔译〕满清王朝建立与维持的过程中被屠杀的受害者,其数目之巨,欧洲人将永远无从知晓;然而——就他们入侵中国的一切可靠记载、境内对其权威此起彼伏的反抗、以及他们所施行的杀戮统治来判断——其生命的毁灭,远超有史以来最大的人类毁灭者,自亚历山大大帝以至成吉思汗,在其行军路上所献祭的那些人众。满清统治之野蛮,古今历史上无可比拟;而他们以酷刑施罚的那种恶魔般的性质——尤其是用于叛逆之罪的那些——以及由他们设立的"刑部"的记录,构成了人类史册上最黑的一块污点。

**语境与保留**:林德利不是旁观者。他曾为太平天国驾驶蒸汽船"飞而复来"(Firefly),被清军与英国海军追捕;此书写于天京陷落后两年,是一份控诉状——控诉英国政府背弃太平天国、助清剿灭之。因此:

- 他笔下必须一边是"伏地之豕"的顺民,一边是眼中有光的太平军;这个对照越强,索还道义的理由越足。"剃发为奴隶徽记"一类语汇直接承自太平天国自身的宣传("满妖"、"薙发之辱")。
- 屠戮那段自相矛盾:先说"欧洲人永远无从知晓",随即排名超过亚历山大与成吉思汗。且其计数把入关之役、此后一切反抗、日常统治全部计入——最大的一笔正是他本人参与的太平天国战争(死亡约两千万),而击败太平天国的力量中有英国的炮舰与洋枪队。这是诉状的算法,不是史家的算法。
- 事实底线:明清易代确为极大屠杀(扬州、嘉定、广州、四川),但明末的内乱、饥荒与瘟疫占很大比重,不能尽归满洲;蒙古西征加灭金灭宋,量级同在数千万。"considerably outnumbered"属不可证亦不可驳的修辞。
- 他对太平天国的理想化亦经不起检验:洪秀全的神权、天王府的排场、杨韦内讧、焚书毁庙、破城屠戮。

此书的价值不在他的判断,在他记下的见闻,以及它本身作为一份史料——它让人看见 1860 年代英国内部为中国这场战争争执到什么程度。

**后续影响**:"满清屠戮甲于古今"这套说法,四十年后成为晚清排满革命的核心语汇(邹容《革命军》、章太炎一路),材料多取自《明季稗史》中扬州、嘉定诸记。此一论述自英国的太平党人流入中国革命党人之手,是值得追索的一条线。

## 三、罗兰(Henry Augustus Rowland),《为纯科学呼吁》,1883 年 8 月 15 日,美国科学促进会(AAAS)物理组演说

> Should we stop its progress, and attend only to its applications, we should soon degenerate into a people like the Chinese, who have made no progress for generations, because they have been satisfied with the applications of science, and have never sought for reasons in what they have done.

——〔译〕我们若停下科学本身的进步,只顾它的应用,不久就会退化成中国人那样的民族——他们世世代代毫无进步,因为他们满足于科学的应用,从来不去追问自己所做之事的道理。

结句:

> we now regard this oldest and most numerous of nations as only barbarians.

——〔译〕我们如今只把这个最古老、人口最多的民族看作野蛮人而已。

**语境**:这篇讲演不是在骂中国,是在骂美国。1883 年的美国盛产实用发明而不出基础理论(爱迪生、贝尔的时代),大学不养理论物理,国会只肯为看得见的用处掏钱。罗兰是约翰·霍普金斯第一位物理学教授、后来美国物理学会首任会长;他要向同行与资助者证明纯科学的价值,"中国"在文中的功能是一记恐吓:**你们再这样下去,就会变成他们那样。**

**一百年来的中国用法**:中国科学界反复引用此文,同样是拿它吓自己人——我们不能只要应用不要基础。用法完全一样,只是这一次被吓的人和被举例的人是同一批人。

**罗兰此说之粗糙**:中国的问题从来不是无人追问所以然(《梦溪笔谈》《天工开物》《本草纲目》皆是;宋元数学的秦九韶、朱世杰一度居世界前列)。真正的断裂是:追问所以然从未成为一份**职业**、一套**机构**、一条**升迁之路**。巴罗 p. 263 说得比罗兰准确——科举所试只在语言文字,而学问"对于取得最高官位并非必需",于是聪明人都去背书了。这不是好奇心的缺席,是激励结构的配置。

## 四、罗素(Bertrand Russell),《中国问题》(*The Problem of China*),1922,第十二章《中国人的性格》,原书 211 页

> the three chief defects of Chinese character are, I should say, avarice, cowardice and callousness.

——〔译〕依我看,中国人性格上的三大缺陷是:贪婪、怯懦、冷漠。

> They have none of that humanitarian impulse which leads us to devote one per cent. of our energy to mitigating the evils wrought by the other ninety-nine per cent.

——〔译〕他们完全没有我们那种人道主义冲动——正是这种冲动,使我们肯拿出百分之一的精力,去减轻另外百分之九十九的精力所造成的祸害。

1920–21 年华北大饥荒,他亲眼所见:

> millions were dying of famine; men sold their children into slavery for a few dollars, and killed them if this sum was unobtainable.

——〔译〕数百万人正死于饥荒;人们为了几块钱把自己的孩子卖作奴婢,若连这几块钱也换不到,就把孩子杀掉。

> Much was done by white men to relieve the famine, but very little by the Chinese, and that little vitiated by corruption.

——〔译〕赈济这场饥荒,白人做了很多,中国人做得极少,而这极少的部分又被贪腐败坏了。

同一页上:

> on the balance, I think the Chinese one of the best nations I have come across.

——〔译〕综合权衡,我认为中国人是我所遇到过的最好的民族之一。

**与巴罗的对应**——三项对三项:

| 罗素(1922) | 巴罗(1804) |
|---|---|
| cowardice 怯懦 | timid(p. 160);「among the most timid people on the face of the earth」——地球上最胆怯的民族之一(p. 178) |
| callousness 冷漠 | indifferent, unfeeling(p. 160);见溺不救(p. 167)、婴尸浮江(p. 171) |
| avarice 贪婪 | 商人必骗、农民必偷、亲王宰相必掠(p. 179–180);赈银层层剥蚀(p. 607) |

**语境与两点保留**:

- 罗素的结构是:三宗恶德是真的,而它们是贫困与恶政的产物;因此他仍能在同一页写下"中国是我所见过最好的民族之一",并相信条件一变它们就会消失。他喜欢中国人而仍作此判断,这比厌恶而作此判断可信得多。
- 但两人不是完全独立的证人。罗素 1920 年来华之前读过十九世纪那整套"专制与国民性"的文献,"专制造成怯懦与冷漠"这一框架在他动笔前已经备好。观察一致,部分可能来自共用的镜头。这不推翻他的记述(饥荒是亲见),但这份"跨世纪吻合"不能当作双盲实验的结果来引用。

## 五、爱因斯坦(Albert Einstein),远东旅行日记,1922–23(普林斯顿大学出版社 2018 年首次公开出版)

日记中称中国人"勤劳、污秽、迟钝"(industrious, filthy, obtuse),并写下若这些人取代其他种族将是一件遗憾的事。

**为何单独列出并加警告**:这一条与上述全部文本性质不同。前面所有人——巴罗、马戛尔尼、达尔文、林德利、罗素——把所见归因于制度、法律、贫困、训练;爱因斯坦谈的是**血统与种族优劣**。这是整份名单里唯一真正落入种族主义的一条,因此**不能**用它来支持"这是观察而非偏见"的论证;引用它只会削弱论证。要证明"这是观察不是偏见",巴罗 p. 176(同样的中国人在南洋以诚实勤勉著称)比爱因斯坦有力一百倍。

## 六、达尔文(Charles Darwin),《voyage of beagle》,1839(巴西一章)

一个意外的关联

> I may mention one very trifling anecdote, which at the time struck me more forcibly than any story of cruelty. I was crossing a ferry with a negro, who was uncommonly stupid. In endeavouring to make him understand, I talked loud, and made signs, in doing which I passed my hand near his face. He, I suppose, thought I was in a passion, and was going to strike him; for instantly, with a frightened look and half-shut eyes, he dropped his hands. I shall never forget my feelings of surprise, disgust, and shame, at seeing a great powerful man afraid even to ward off a blow, directed, as he thought, at his face. This man had been trained to a degradation lower than the slavery of the most helpless animal.

——〔译〕我要说一件极小的事,它当时给我的震动,超过任何一桩残酷的故事。我与一个黑人同乘渡船,那人异常迟钝。我想让他听懂,便提高了声音,又比划手势,这样一来,手就从他脸旁掠过。他大概以为我动了怒、要打他;因为他立刻露出惊惧的神色,半闭着眼,垂下了双手。一个高大有力的汉子,连挡一下他以为将要落在自己脸上的拳头都不敢——看到这一幕时我那种惊讶、厌恶与羞耻的感觉,我永生不会忘记。这个人是被训练成了一种比最无助的畜生之为奴还要更低的卑屈状态。

**为何要与巴罗并读**:这段写的不是中国,是巴西的一个非洲奴隶,而结论用的是与巴罗完全相同的词——**trained**,不是 born,不是 by nature。两人隔着半个地球、四十年,记下同一个动作与同一个成因。这是"畏缩不属于某个民族,而属于任意权力"的最有力旁证。

另注意两处:达尔文特别写明那是"a great powerful man"——力量在他身上,只是被训练成不许使用;以及,达尔文的**羞耻是施加者一方的羞耻**——让他羞耻的不是那人的怯懦,是自己抬起的那只手,和造出这只手的整套制度。这一章的结尾是"我感谢上帝,我将永不再访问一个奴隶之国",而他愤怒的首要对象是自己的文明与同胞。

# 第四部分 结语:讨论中确立的几条判断

**一、这些记述的可靠性,恰恰建立在它们不是种族论上。**
巴罗写 "by nature quiet, passive, and timid",恶德是 the state of society and the abuse of the laws 造成的;达尔文写 trained;罗素归因于贫困与恶政。三人都在说:这是权力对人做的事,不是某个民族的本性。承认史实而不接受种族结论,不是回避,而是这些文本自身的逻辑。

**二、对照组始终存在。**
南洋华人(巴罗 p. 176)、受杖抗辩的旗人(p. 381–382)、林德利笔下与"顺民"截然不同的太平军——同一批人,换掉压在头上的东西,一代之内就是另一副样子。这是全部材料中最硬的一条。

**三、明清之别:清朝的独特贡献是把文字之祸变成了一套长期运转的机构。**
跪对君臣、廷杖大臣、八股取士、海禁、以文字杀人,几乎全是明朝的发明;明初的酷烈是皇帝个人的暴怒,人死事止。清朝的不同在三处:连续性与数量(顺治函可案起,康熙庄廷鑨《明史》案、戴名世《南山集》案,雍正吕留良案开棺枭首,乾隆一朝百三十余起为最烈);专门的机器(修《四库全书》同时禁毁约三千种书,禁书目录、各省缴书、地方官考成,明朝无可比者);以及一个明朝不可能有的动力——**合法性焦虑**:少数征服集团统治一个讲华夷之辨的文明,任何涉及夷、胡、虏、明、故国的字眼都自动带有政治危险,禁忌的边界因此无法预知,而无法预知的禁忌远比明确的禁令有效,因为人只能自我阉割到远超安全线的地方。

最硬的证据:黄宗羲《明夷待访录》、顾炎武的书清代长期不能公开流传,《明夷待访录》要到晚清才被重印,梁启超、孙中山拿它当传单散发。**这个文明自己产出的最深刻的政治批判,被埋了二百多年。** 晚明还有王阳明、李贄、东林、黄顾这样一条活的批判传统;清朝的成就是把它掐断了。

**四、不是"行尸走肉",是"改道"。**
清代仍出曹雪芹、吴敬梓、戴震、章学诚、袁枚,乾嘉考据学水准极高——而钱穆、余英时都指出,考据学的兴盛本身就是文字狱的产物:义理与时政不能碰,天下最聪明的头脑于是转去校勘一个字的音韵源流,做了两百年。这个说法比"行尸走肉"更难受,因为它意味着才智并未消失,它被完整地保存下来,只是被引到了一条绝对安全也绝对无关的河道里。这正是巴罗 p. 263 的内容。

**五、1921 年那道坎。**
罗素看到卖儿、杀儿、赈款贪没,是 1921 年——清朝已亡九年,辫子剪了,皇帝退位了,共和建立了。一百二十七年后,同一张清单一字不改地又开了一次。这只有两种解释:或者那套机制的寿命远长于制造它的王朝,或者它从来就不只是那个王朝制造的。"这是满清近三百年统治的后果"这一论断,在这里遇到最难越过的一道坎。

**六、"满清野蛮"这个叙事本身有它的政治功能。**
它成形于 1900 年代排满革命党人之手,作用很清楚:把病灶安放在"外族"身上,中国自身的制度便不必受审。这是一种让羞耻可以被承受的安排——保留了羞耻,免除了追问。更难但更准确的说法是:那套让人跪下谢恩的机制是这个文明自己长出来的,清朝把它用到了极致。这也解释了为什么 1912 年之后那些东西并没有随着辫子一起消失。

**七、羞愧的方向。**
能为跪着感到羞耻,本身是很晚才有的能力。1793 年运河边跪下谢恩的那个人不觉得羞耻,他觉得那是本分;今人觉得羞耻,是因为已经先认定人不该跪。所以这份痛不是那段历史的延续,是它已被走出一段距离的证据——羞耻由差距造成,没有差距就没有羞耻。

剩下的只是方向:朝后看,它会变成对自己人的鄙夷,或"我们就是这样"的认命,两者都是巴罗那半句错话的回声;朝前看,它是一种警觉——因为他描述的机制没有一条是清朝独有的:惩罚被称作恩典;告到上一级无门;没有能与权力讲价的中间阶层;人们从未想到自己拥有权利(p. 396,「it has never occurred to them that they have any rights」——〔译〕他们从来没有想到过自己拥有任何权利)。这些零件不姓爱新觉罗,它们属于任意权力,在任何时代任何国家只要条件具备就会重新装配起来。认得出它们,是读这些书最实在的收获。
