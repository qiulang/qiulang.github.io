In December 1980 Hayek wrote a letter to Samuelson stating that his textbook Economics was a source of the "false allegation…which I constantly encounter, most resent and can only regard as a malicious distortion which has largely succeeded in discrediting my argument…," namely, the allegation that Hayek believed that "each step away from the market system and towards social reform of the welfare state is inevitably a journey that must end in a totalitarian state." In a reply Samuelson apologized and promised to represent Hayek's views more accurately in any future work.

### socialism 和 socialists

我读这本书其实有个和别人不一样感受 （至少我没读到别人有个疑问），书里说的 socialism 和 socialists 到底指什么？比如书的开篇 To the socialists of all parties . 我上学的年代，社会主义就是被简单粗暴分成空想社会主义和科学社会主义，虽然后来知道这是我们最喜欢的简单粗暴二分法，但也一直没有仔细想过这个问题，一直到读到 这本书

> （献词）致所有党派的社会主义者

书里对 socialism 描述对我是很有冲击力的，举几个例子：

第一章 "Although we had been warned by some of the greatest political thinkers of the nineteenth century, by de Tocqueville and Lord Acton, that socialism means slavery…"

> 尽管十九世纪一些最伟大的政治思想家——托克维尔和阿克顿勋爵——曾警告过我们，**社会主义意味着奴役**……

第二章 "that socialism has displaced liberalism as the doctrine held by the great majority of progressives does not simply mean that people had forgotten the warnings of the great liberal thinkers of the past"

> 社会主义取代自由主义，成为绝大多数进步人士所奉持的学说——这件事**并不仅仅意味着**人们忘记了过去那些伟大的自由主义思想家的警告

about the consequences of collectivism.The extraordinary thing is that the same socialism that was not only early recognised as the gravest threat to freedom, but quite openly began as a reaction against the liberalism of the French Revolution, gained general acceptance under the flag of liberty."

> ……（那些警告是）关于集体主义之后果的。**离奇之处在于**：同一个社会主义，不但很早就被认出是对自由最严重的威胁，而且它一开始就公开地是**对法国大革命之自由主义的一种反动**——它却打着**自由的旗号**赢得了普遍接受。

第三章  individualism and collectivism 章节开始引语 "The socialists believe in two things which are absolutely different and perhaps even contradictory: freedom and organization."

> 社会主义者信奉两样绝然不同、甚至可能互相矛盾的东西：**自由**与**组织**。

第五章 And to make it quite clear that a socialist government must not allow itself to be too much fettered by democratic procedure,

> 而且，为了把话说得十分清楚：一个社会主义政府**决不能让自己被民主程序束缚得太厉害**，

第七章 In their wishful belief that there is really no longer an economic problem people have been confirmed by irresponsible talk about "potential plenty"— which, if it were a fact, would indeed mean that there is no economic problem which makes the choice inevitable. But although this snare has served socialist propaganda under various names as long as socialism has existed, it is still as palpably untrue as it was when it was first used over a hundred years ago.

> 人们一厢情愿地相信经济问题真的已经不复存在，而关于"**潜在的富足**"的那些不负责任的说法又强化了这种信念——倘若那真是事实，就的确意味着不再存在那种**使取舍成为必然**的经济问题。但这个圈套虽然自社会主义诞生之日起就以各种名目为社会主义宣传效力，它今天**仍然像一百多年前初次被使用时一样，明显地不真实**。

### 最好的章节和最弱的章节

回到这本书，我个人觉得最好章节是 第六章 PLANNING AND THE RULE OF LAW ，对这章的评论很多，我就不展开，但是我觉得论证最弱是第十章 WHY THE WORST GET ON TOP

这章节 说 It seems to be almost a law of human nature that it is easier for people to agree on a negative program—on the hatred of an enemy, on the envy of those better off— than on any positive task. The contrast between the "we" and the "they," the common fight against those outside the group, seems to be an essential ingredient in any creed which will solidly knit together a group for common action. 这句我有很深印象。而且现实到例子就是 Donald Trump 很熟练运用这个 "we" and the "they" 煽动对立，但是但是怎么得出 Why the Worst Get on Top 的论证我觉得很不充分的。

> 这似乎几乎是一条人性的法则：**人们更容易就一个否定性的纲领达成一致——对一个敌人的仇恨，对境况更好者的嫉妒——而不是就任何肯定性的任务达成一致。**"我们"与"他们"的对立，共同对抗群体之外的那些人，似乎是任何**能把一群人牢固地拧在一起以采取共同行动**的信条中，不可或缺的成分。
